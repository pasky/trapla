Trapla

About Trapla and its data

Trapla is a game about designing roads and railways on real Czech terrain: the cheapest line the design rules allow, priced the way road builders price one. It is a prototype, free, with no ads and no tracking. It keeps your designs in your own browser only.

Prices, design rules and noise follow Czech sources (SFDI price lists, ČSN 73 6101, the Czech noise manual and others), cited one by one in the game's source code. What the game makes of them is a game, not a design study.
