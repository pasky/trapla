# Minor at-grade crossroads: marginal construction cost (Czech Republic)

Research memo; CZK, construction cost excluding VAT where the source states it. The target is the **extra** cost of shaping a basic at-grade connection beyond the two road pavements already priced as their ordinary running alignment. No signals, turn lanes, roundabout, utility relocation, major drainage work, or long approach reconstruction. Prices are nominal source-year prices; no inflation conversion is applied.

## Findings

| Case | Recommended extra cost | Evidence / calculation | Confidence |
|---|---:|---|---|
| (a) X/T junction of two local roads (MO/M 7.5 class, built-up local street) | **CZK 1,000,000** | **PROVISIONAL.** No directly priced, separable ordinary local-street X/T junction found. A 2025 municipal-infrastructure cost guide gives illustrative local-road construction rates around CZK 2,552–5,729/m², plus about CZK 885–1,236 per metre for concrete kerbs; a compact junction's extra paved/edge-treatment footprint is only a few hundred square metres/metres, so the incremental amount is on the order of CZK 0.5–1.5m, not millions. Rounded midpoint. | **Low** (transparent area-based estimate; no observed junction award) |
| (b) Field/forest track crossing/joining another track or local road (simple P3/P4, no major culvert) | **CZK 250,000** | **PROVISIONAL.** The 2023 KoPÚ Sněhov plan has individually costed track works of CZK 294,450–770,250 for 154–394 m new/low-standard paths (roughly CZK 1,250–1,880/m² based on listed area); it does not price a junction separately. A modest widened/graded connection area of roughly 100–150 m² plus simple edge/earthwork treatment therefore supports a low hundreds-of-thousands allowance. A track tie-in to an existing paved public road requiring drainage/culvert or pavement cut should instead retain the existing **CZK 500,000** sjezd allowance. | **Very low** (inferred from whole-path budgets; no itemised junction cost) |

**Bottom line:** the sources provide better unit-cost context than the old placeholders but do **not** give a clean marginal-price observation for either type. The existing CZK 7m tie-in is not a good direct proxy for (a): it concerns a larger class-III/local tie-in to a rebuilt higher-class road and its whole junction scope. The existing CZK 500k access/sjezd remains a sensible conservative comparison for a track connection onto an existing public road; this memo recommends CZK 250k for the simpler track-to-track case, but with very low confidence.

## 1. Why a local-road junction is much less than the old CZK 7m tie-in

The requested case is not a new road connection to a major highway, nor a large junction reconstruction. It is a compact, low-speed local X/T where the main road pavements are separately charged. The marginal work is principally the local flare/turning geometry, extra surface area, edge construction, and modest traffic-control work. Neither the 2025 guide nor tender searches located an isolated price for that exact scope, so a single game value cannot honestly be labelled sourced.

The MMR/ÚÚR *Průměrné ceny dopravní a technické infrastruktury obcí*, update 2025, reports indicative unit costs based on ÚRS 2025/I and worked local-road examples. It is not a tender award or a junction schedule. Extracts relevant to scale:

> “D07 … Místní obslužná přístupová šířky 8,6 m, 8 m a 7 m – dvoupruhová s kolmým stáním a parkovištěm”
>
> “Celkem v CÚ roku … 2025 … 14 227 … Kč/m² …”

That D07 includes parking, so it is not a clean road-only rate. The more basic worked D08 states:

> “D08 … Místní obslužná přístupová šířky 7 m – dvoupruhová s oboustranným chodníkem”
>
> “Celkem v CÚ roku … 2025 … 5 729 … Kč/m²”

D08 also includes sidewalks and excludes separate lighting/drainage, making CZK 5,729/m² a useful upper-context benchmark rather than a direct pavement rate. D11, an ÚRS 2025 access-road example, reports CZK 3,468/m²:

> “D11 … Účelová šířky 6 m a 5 m – dvoupruhová”
>
> “Celkem v CÚ roku … 2025 … 8 288 … tis. Kč … 2 390 … m² … 3 468 Kč/m²”

The guide also quotes ordinary concrete kerbs at:

> “obrubníky betonové – montované … bm … 885”
>
> “obrubníky betonové – montované do průřezu 0,05 m² … bm … 1 236”

**Marginal-area arithmetic (estimate, not a measured quantity):** At about CZK 3,500–5,700/m², 100–200 m² of extra paved footprint costs roughly CZK 0.35–1.14m before additional edge/marking/signage work. Add perhaps 20–40 m of kerb/edge work at the cited rates (about CZK 18k–49k), and a modest allowance for markings/signs and drainage details. This makes CZK 1m a reasonable compact-junction midpoint. The geometry range is an explicit game assumption; the guide does not price a specific junction layout. Some minor streets have no kerbs, while urban curb-and-gutter or utility/drainage works can push costs higher.

Source: [MMR/ÚÚR, *Průměrné ceny dopravní a technické infrastruktury obcí*, 2025 PDF](https://www.uur.cz/media/y1ulzdvg/08-komunikace-ceny-ti-2025.pdf), chapter 8, pp. 108–123 (visited; full PDF text extracted). Its worked examples are explicitly described as based on “rozpočtové ukazatele a ceníky” / KROS 4 2025/I ÚRS; the guide is not an isolated contract price.

## 2. Tracks: real KoPÚ budgets, but no separate connection item

A useful public, itemised KoPÚ source is the *Plán společných zařízení KoPÚ Sněhov* (March 2023), published by Malá Skála. It provides lengths, areas and **expected costs without VAT** for individual field/track projects. The table does not isolate crossing or connection costs, and several entries are reconstruction rather than new build.

Verbatim cost-table extract (the PDF text extraction has some column wrapping):

> “DC2-R … doplňková 3,0 … 394 … 1 580 … 770 250”
>
> “DC18 … doplňková 3,0 … 154 … 754 … nově navržená … 294 450”
>
> “DC16 … doplňková 3,0 … 399 … 1 654 … nově navržená … 388 050”
>
> “VC1-R … vedlejší 4,0/20 … 695 … 3 868 … stávající k rekonstrukci … 2 871 960”
>
> “Označení cesty … předpokládané náklady … bez DPH”

For DC18 and DC16, costs divided by listed areas are respectively about CZK 390/m² and CZK 235/m² (these particularly low sums reflect low-standard grassy/track treatments and are not a paved-road construction rate). DC2-R comes to about CZK 488/m². The higher-standard VC1-R is about CZK 742/m². Whole-path unit costs are not identical to a junction surfacing cost: route earthworks, drainage, reconstruction, and surface differ. This source strongly supports that ordinary grass/gravel field tracks are on a far lower cost scale than the paved local-road examples, but **does not publish a crossing price**.

The same plan shows what is meant by physical connections:

> “DC14-R … Začátek trasy je na místní komunikaci MK1 a vede severním směrem…”
>
> “DC15 … Začátek trasy se nachází na nově navržené polní cestě DC16.”
>
> “DC19 … Trasa navržené cesty začíná na silnici III/28722 … Končí na místní komunikaci.”

These are design descriptions, not priced junction line-items. The overall plan gives “Opatření ke zpřístupnění pozemků” total CZK 22,243,910, but that covers an entire set of roads, not a connection.

Source: [KoPÚ Sněhov, *Plán společných zařízení* PDF](https://www.mala-skala.cz/evt_file.php?file=6629), March 2023, especially pp. 19–22 and 39 (visited; full document text extracted).

A second SPÚ procurement illustrates the usual missing distinction: the 2023 HPC 1 project in Pláně u Plas has a published estimated contract value of CZK 17,948,300 excl. VAT, but the description says the existing tie-in to the class-III road is reused with only pavement maintenance:

> “Pro napojení polní cesty na začátku úpravy na silnici III. třídy bude využito stávající komunikační napojení bez větších stavebních úprav, bude provedena pouze údržba krytu.”

This is not a junction cost and cannot calibrate a new connection. It is useful evidence that where an existing usable access exists, SPÚ may assume little or no new junction work. The work itself is a P5.0/30, asphalt-paved reconstruction with drainage and earthworks; its total is emphatically not a price for the tie-in.

Source: [SPÚ E-ZAK, *Stavba polní cesty HPC 1 v k.ú. Pláně u Plas*](https://zakazky.spucr.cz/contract_display_42580.html), procurement opened 2 May 2023 (visited; detailed scope and estimate shown).

## 3. Decision and limitations

- **Local-road X/T: CZK 1m extra, PROVISIONAL, low confidence.** This replaces the inappropriate use of a CZK 7m major-road tie-in for this narrowly defined local/local junction, but it is a quantity-based estimate, not a measured Czech junction price. Price the added junction footprint only; do not charge the full intersection again if both roads' ordinary pavement area is already billed.
- **Track connection: CZK 250k extra for track-to-track/simple low-speed joining, PROVISIONAL, very low confidence.** The KoPÚ evidence validates the lower cost scale, not that exact connection amount. Use the existing **CZK 500k** for a track joining a local road when it needs a formed paved sjezd, culvert/drainage, or repair of the existing road edge. If game logic cannot distinguish these cases, keep CZK 500k as the conservative single access figure rather than claim new empirical support.
- A crossing that needs a new culvert, drainage tie-in, pavement reconstruction, utility relocation, kerbs/sidewalks, or significant regrading is outside these simple allowances. Price such scope separately. No usable source found that itemises a simple field-track junction or local/local junction as a separable construction object.
