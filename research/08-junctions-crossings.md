# Junctions, crossings and road-network rules (Czechia)

Research memo for a new S 9.5 class-I road (80–90 km/h, ~9,000 veh/day) near Trutnov; CZK excl. VAT, 2025/26 level. **Confidence** refers to evidence for the proposed value, not legal/design approval. Costs include uncertainty in project scope; project awards are not clean unit rates. Norm text is generally paid; do not treat paraphrases below as quotations from a paid standard.

**Law quotations (short extracts):** Act 13/1997 §10(1) says roads may be connected “zřizováním křižovatek” or adjoining properties by “sjezdů nebo nájezdů.” Decree 104/1997 §12(1) says “Sjezdy a nájezdy … lze zřídit, jen pokud …” (followed by safety/operation conditions). These provisions establish permitting and conditions; neither quote by itself establishes a spacing number or bans every at-grade junction on class I.

## Traffic/category thresholds

- ČSN 73 6101 (2018) is the current rural road/motorway design standard (catalog: [ČAS record](https://csnonline.agentura-cas.cz/Detailnormy.aspx?k=505692)); its full text is paid. [VUT teaching material applying ČSN 73 6101](https://www.fce.vutbr.cz/pko/sad/pdf/aplikace.pdf) says categories correspond to *návrhové hodnoty intenzit* and category changes are handled at junctions, but does not establish the current table values in the indexed extract.
- No primary accessible source verified an AADT table supporting the project's `<5,000 ≤ S 9.5 <12,000` cutoffs or a 4-lane threshold. Mark all those thresholds **UNVERIFIED**, not normative facts. Daily traffic must be converted to the standard's design-hour/forecast basis, with composition, growth and design period; category and lane count also depend on capacity/level of service and junction performance.
- Recommendation: retain S 9.5 for the stated scenario as a **scenario assumption** (9,000 veh/day), confidence **low-medium**, until the category table in current ČSN 73 6101 or authorized traffic-design evidence is checked. Do not revise numeric thresholds from old web tables or the older (2004) norm.

## Vertical clearance and overbridge threshold

- ČSN 73 6201 clearance: **4.80 m** for motorway and class I/II roads; **4.50 m** for class III and local roads, according to [ŘSD low-clearance sign guidance R 111](https://www.rsd.cz/documents/38144/57719/R+111+_Vzory+zna%C4%8Den%C3%AD+_N%C3%ADzk%C3%A9+a+%C3%BAzk%C3%A9+podjezdy.pdf/b67631e6-6660-b267-446e-e607a3bd3d45?t=1726659829807) (ŘSD document, publication date not stated; accessed in this research). It attributes those values to ČSN 73 6201. The implementing [Decree 104/1997 §15](https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/1997-104) ties the clearance envelope to binding ČSN 73 6201. Thus use 4.8m for the scenario's class-I road, **not 4.5m**.
- No independently confirmed universal extra reserve or deck structural depth was found. The model may use 5.0m clear (0.2m allowance) and 1.0–1.5m bridge depth as explicit heuristics; both are **UNVERIFIED** as fixed rules. Combined grade difference ~5.8–6.5m explains why 6m is a plausible crossing-classification cutoff; it is not itself a ČSN clearance requirement.

## Implementation takeaways

1. Replace all-in through-junction placeholder 12m with **25m (15–35m)** and keep roundabout as separate **32m (20–40m)** item; mark low confidence and permit scenario-specific overrides.
2. Keep 4m / 0.5m / 1m reconnection prices as low-confidence game allowances; do not describe them as observed Czech averages.
3. Use bridge rate `A.2.S.6,5.N` for a 6.5m local-road bridge; calculate cost by span × width × ~66.5k CZK/m² standard SPK proxy. Scale up/down with actual deck width, not road classification label alone.
4. For a road crossing beneath a bridge, enforce 4.8m clear for the class-I road, with optional 0.2m simulation reserve. Treat the 6m road-grade threshold as a heuristic incorporating deck depth.
5. Network topology: prohibit at-grade crossings of motorways; permit class-I at-grade junctions only if approved/designed as such; never make private sjezdy automatic road junctions. Exact junction spacing and AADT category thresholds stay **UNVERIFIED** pending lawful access to current ČSN texts.
