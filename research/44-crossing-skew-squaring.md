# Crossing angle and bend radii of short realignments — Czech sources

Research date: 2026-11-16 (research subagent; translated from its Czech write-up). ČSN norms change;
where the full current text could not be checked this says so. "Crossing angle" (the angle between the
two road axes) is not always the same as a bridge's "skew" (deck axis against the normal to the
obstacle); each value below states what it measures.

## 1. Crossing angle

| Subject | Finding / short quote | Source and confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Bridge over a road — limit in a public ŘSD document | ŘSD lists "křížení komunikace a mostu menší než 70 °" (a road and a bridge crossing at less than 70°) as a case needing special sign placement and hatching of the underpass. Angles under 70° are therefore non-standard for signing; this alone does **not** prove that ČSN 73 6201 forbids more skewed bridges. Measured between the axes: at least 70° (77.8 gon), i.e. at most 20° from square. | ŘSD, *R 111 – Vzory značení, Nízké a úzké podjezdy*, [PDF](https://www.rsd.cz/documents/38144/57719/R+111+_Vzory+zna%C4%8Den%C3%AD+_N%C3%ADzk%C3%A9+a+%C3%BAzk%C3%A9+podjezdy.pdf/b67631e6-6660-b267-446e-e607a3bd3d45?t=1726659829807), p. 4; **high** that the signing limit exists, **low** as a design minimum. |
| ČSN 73 6201 — fixed minimum | No publicly accessible full text of a clause giving a universal minimum bridge angle or "as square as possible" was found, so neither can be quoted. Czech projects report skew as a bridge parameter (e.g. "100,0g (90,0°) – most je kolmý"). Larger skew lengthens the structure along the deck and complicates abutments and bearings: a geometric and structural consequence, not a normative number. | [ČSN 73 6201 preview](http://csnonlinefirmy.agentura-cas.cz/html_nahledy/73/17145/17145_nahled.htm); project example ([Středočeský kraj](https://zakazky.kr-stredocesky.cz/document_101297/3bb5cc4bba6efc32f201d6d6068bcce7-201_01_technicka_zprava-pdf)); **low** for a normative duty, **high** for the geometry. |
| At-grade junction — ČSN 73 6102 | Teaching material reproducing the norm: "křižující se pozemní komunikace mají svírat pravý úhel, který nesmí být menší než 75 stupňů a větší než 105 stupňů" (crossing roads form a right angle, not below 75° nor above 105°). Axis angle; not automatically a bridge skew limit. | ČZU teaching material [Uspořádání křižovatek](https://home.czu.cz/storage/929/158640_04-Fanta-Doprava-2.pdf); [ČSN 73 6102 preview](http://csnonlinefirmy.agentura-cas.cz/html_nahledy/73/79325/79325_nahled.htm); **medium**. |
| Grade separation / realigning a field track or road | ŘSD documents routinely realign crossed paths and keep access, but no general Czech rule was found ordering every crossed field track to be squared, nor a "maximum skew" before realigning. Using the junction range 75–105° as a binding bridge limit is unsupported. | e.g. ŘSD D55 5501 [text part](https://md.gov.cz/getattachment/c5046f40-99cb-4f58-a3d7-86fdcb899dbe/priloha-MD-7714_2025-940_15-textova-cast.pdf.aspx); **low** (negative search). |
| International (not a Czech rule) | UK DMRB CD 350: "All bridges with a skew angle up to 30 degrees or 60m length or less shall be designed as integral bridge structures" (scope of a design requirement, not a ban). A Michigan DOT report: skew 30–45° needs refined analysis, above 45° further special procedures. | [DMRB CD 350](https://www.standardsforhighways.co.uk/tses/attachments/19858eae-6dd2-4669-90a7-38aa8c85a1dd?inline=true); [MDOT, Skewed Highway Bridges](https://rosap.ntl.bts.gov/view/dot/26216/dot_26216_DS1.pdf); **high** for the quoted scope, not applicable to the Czech Republic. |

**Effect of skew:** the deck (or box) crosses the obstacle over its width divided by the sine of the
crossing angle (at 60° 1.155 × the square length); skewed supports need skewed cross beams/abutments and
carry uneven reactions.

## Adopted in the game (cost model 24)

- Auto weighs straightening a crossed road that meets ours at **less than 70°** (`SQUARE_BELOW`, the ŘSD
  R 111 non-standard threshold; a game rule, not a Czech minimum bridge angle) and keeps the cheaper
  buildable variant; the new line crosses at a right angle (the junction norm's target of 90°).
- Bends of the new line are no sharper than `minRadius`: tracks 25 m (30 km/h), service roads and local
  streets 27 m (ČSN 73 6110, 30 km/h, 2.5 %), class I–III roads 90 m (50 km/h, 7 %); the design speeds
  are PROVISIONAL choices for a short realignment next to a grade separation.
