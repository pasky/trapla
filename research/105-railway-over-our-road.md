# New railway bridge over a new road: sourced design inputs

Research note for a new road passing in a cutting beneath an existing railway carried by a replacement/new bridge. Sources opened 2026-11-16; standard-derived values below are reported from publicly accessible explanatory material, not substituted for the current licensed ČSN text.

## Findings at a glance

- **Road clearance:** 4.80 m vertical traffic envelope for motorways and class I–II roads; 4.50 m for class III roads and faster/collector local roads; 4.20 m for local access roads and public-purpose roads. Add **0.15 m** structural safety gap: resulting required underside clearance is respectively **4.95 / 4.65 / 4.35 m**. No source found here for a separate future-resurfacing allowance.
- **SPOŽES 2026:** H01 “Nový železniční most - do 40 m”, m², **0.0868 mil. Kč/m² = 86,800 Kč/m²**; H07 “Mostní provizoria”, m², **0.0398 mil. Kč/m² = 39,800 Kč/m²**. These are study-stage normatives, not tender unit prices.
- **Bridge depth, track-based width, and track centre spacing:** no reliable numerical typical values established in this pass; do not use an unsourced assumed “standard” depth or width.
- **Example / construction method:** ŘSD’s I/27 Přeštice bypass leaflet describes construction of a bridge on the existing railway alignment with two possessions: temporary bridge structures at the abutments during the first, bridge installation during the second. The accessible snippet does not give spans, width, possession lengths or cost.
- **SPOŽES searchable items:** H07 is an explicit provisional-bridge allowance. No separate workbook rows were found by searching for “výluka”, “přeložka koleje”, “provizorní přeložka” or “za provozu”; this is not proof that the whole project estimate has no possession costs (the workbook has separate possession-planning forms).

## Temporary works, traffic and prices

The official 2026 workbook provides a separate **H07 “Mostní provizoria”** area-based allowance. Its possession model is elsewhere: sheet **NAD** (náhradní autobusová doprava) contains fields for individual possessions and formulas; the visible template wording defines Dp as workdays with traffic excluded during one possession and Dv as weekend days. It also states “Sazba za km – rozmezí sazby dle místních podmínek od 85 Kč do 200 Kč” for replacement bus costs. This is not a price for a possession or a bridge; it is a bus-kilometre input. The template includes example possession date ranges (e.g. a 29-day interval) but they are illustrative input examples, not evidence of a representative bridge duration.

For a new road below an existing railway, the Přeštice example supports a plausible staged option with provisional structures and multiple short/long possessions; it does **not** establish that this is always preferable to a full line closure or temporary track diversion. No defensible current cost or typical duration for a ŽMP/KD railway provisional bridge was located.

## Not found / PROVISIONAL

- **Current normative text / table:** ČSN 73 6201 is not freely available in its entirety from the sources inspected. The clearance values are corroborated by a ČSN-explaining university deck and the ŘSD R 111 guidance, not quoted directly from the paid standard.
- **Resurfacing reserve:** not located. Do not conflate the sourced 0.15 m safety gap with a future increase in road level.
- **Bridge depth:** no sourced general dimension from rail top to soffit for short-span ballasted bridges; no build-up verified for ballast, sleeper, rail and deck slab together.
- **Bridge plan width:** no verified single/double-track width or standard clearance between parapets; no confirmed universal 4.0 m track-centre spacing.
- **Construction examples:** one direct match (Přeštice) surfaced, but only the method fragment was available. No span/width, award value or possession duration extracted. A generic new-road-under-railway bridge example with these data remains open.
- **OTSKP:** no relevant railway bridge work-item rates were verified; SPOŽES is the directly relevant study-stage rail-cost source found. Do not use highway OTSKP codes as railway bridge rates without checking scope.
- **“Za provozu” cost premium, temporary track diversion and possession charges:** no distinct matching line items found in the 2026 functional workbook keyword scan. This is a scope/result limitation, not proof such costs are never included under other work packages.

### Practical reading

For realistic short-span ballasted examples found here, **1.3–1.8 m** is defensible as an observed construction-height range for 13.5–16.5 m embedded-beam / steel-concrete decks; a short prefabricated concrete frame can be shallower (observed **0.99 m** at only 4.19 m span). A double-track example reports **11.29 m** overall “width”; a single-track 4.19 m frame reports **9.72 m between railings**. These are sparse project examples, not track-count formulae. No universal width-per-track should be inferred from them.

## Not found (second pass)

- No authoritative SŽ S4, TKP or current ČSN passage located that states a general bridge construction depth from TK to soffit, or a single/double-track deck width.
- No verified Czech minimum parapet/walkway offset from track axis, nor authoritative confirmation of a universal 4.0 m open-line track-centre spacing, from the sources opened in this pass.
- No robust source here for the full additive build-up (rail + sleeper + ballast + waterproofing/protection layer + structural member) to calculate TK-to-soffit independently. The 0.30 m ballast statement is a track-interaction criterion only.
- No sourced generic single-track deck-width range for 10–40 m spans. The one single-track measurement found (9.72 m between railings) is for a 4.19 m-span frame, outside the target span range.
- No directly comparable new railway bridge over a newly built motorway/road cutting with reliable construction cost; the Chomutov thesis is a new two-track railway bridge over a local road but is documented as a rail reconstruction project, and no cost was taken from it.
