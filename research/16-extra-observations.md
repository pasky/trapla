# Older Czech bridge comparators

Research snapshot: 2026-11-14. Supplemental geometry evidence, **not a modern-price calibration sample**. No minimum deck-area filter is applied.

| Bridge | Area basis | Source height | Historical price / caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nuselský, Prague | 485 × 26.5 = **12,852.5 m²** upper road deck; metro in box | **42.5 m**, reported “výška mostu”, datum/maximum not rigorously specified | **152.7 M Kčs**, original total build cost, opened 1973; mixed road/metro structure, not comparable with 2026 CZK |
| Žďákovský, I/19 | Technical article states **7,050 m²**; steel length 541.94 m × overall width 13.5 m gives outer footprint 7,316.19 m², a different convention | About **50 m above reservoir water**, operating level unspecified; not original valley-floor height | **71 M Kčs**, completed 1967, original project total; no modern-price normalization |

## Visited sources and quotations

- [ČT24, Nuselský most slaví (2013)](https://ct24.ceskatelevize.cz/clanek/regiony/nuselsky-most-slavi-298533): “26,5 m – šířka horní mostovky”; “42,5 m – výška mostu”; “152,7 milionu Kčs – celkové náklady na stavbu (původní odhad 86 milionů Kčs).” Basic data attributed to Prague public transport company.
- [ČKAIT, Nuselský most (2014)](https://www.ckait.cz/nuselsky-most-vyjimecna-stavba-v-praze): length 485 m, width 26.5 m, four piers “v průměru více než čtyřicet metrů nad údolím”; corroborates 152.7m final bill. Average pier statement is **not** proof that 42.5 m is a maximum.
- [Rotter & Studnička, Čtyřicet let Žďákovského mostu (2007)](https://www.casopisstavebnictvi.cz/clanky-ctyricet-let-zdakovskeho-mostu.html): “Celková délka ocelové konstrukce mostu je 541,94 m a celková šířka mostu 13,5 m”; explicitly “plocha mostu 7050 m2”. Road 10.5 m + two 1.25 m footways. Preserve the source’s area rather than silently replacing it with the gross product.
- [South Bohemia, Žďákovský most](https://www.jiznicechy.cz/turisticke-cile/2289-zdakovsky-most): “Stavba za 71 milionů korun československých byla definitivně dokončena roku 1967”; “klene se 50 metrů nad hladinou přehrady”. Regional tourism source, rounded dimensions and approximate water datum.

**Excluded false lead:** Trojský’s supplier reports a 34 m height of its load-bearing structure above maximum navigable water. That includes the arch; it is not established as road-deck height or pier height. It must not enter a deck-elevation cost fit. Sources: [Heidelberg](https://www.heidelbergmaterials.cz/cs/referencni-stavby/trojsky-most), [Transportbeton](https://www.transportbeton.cz/vltavu-preklenul-novy-trojsky-most-z-vysokohodnotnych-betonu-ktere-umoznily-vytvorit-subtilni-konstrukci-bez-opory.html).
