# Czech field- and forest-track design values (research note)

**Scope / confidence.** Values below are transcribed from L. Vébr’s 2022 presentation on ČSN 73 6109:2013 and the publicly available *final draft* of ČSN 73 6108 (2016), not the licensed 2018 forest-road standard. The presentation is a secondary source; its slides reproduce the cited standard tables. Forest figures therefore describe the draft, and must not be silently presented as verified 2018 requirements. PDF page numbers below are the printed page numbers (presentation slide number; forest draft pagination). Confidence reflects source clarity, not legal status.

Sources: [Vébr, Revize ČSN 73 6109 (PDF)](https://konference-projektovani.cz/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/10-vebr.pdf); [ČSN 73 6108 final draft (PDF)](https://www.cmadz.cz/aktuality/files/1458303301_CSN_73_6108_-_konecny_navrh_4_s_prijatymi_upravami.pdf); [MZe, Technická doporučení pro projektování lesní dopravní sítě (PDF)](https://mze.gov.cz/public/web/file/658791/Technicka_doporuceni_projektovani_lesni_dopravni_site_A4_WEB.pdf) (secondary guidance, not the standard). The supplied ČVUT slides were unavailable at the supplied host; web search surfaced a moved CVUT URL: https://storm.fsv.cvut.cz/data/files/p%C5%99edm%C4%9Bty/GPU/prednasky%20GPU/GPU2016_polni_cesty_bez_obr.pdf .

## Field tracks — ČSN 73 6109:2013

| Topic | Value / interpretation | Source, short Czech quote | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recommended categories (Table 1, p. 11) | Main, two-lane: **P 6,0/30**. Main, one-lane: **P 4,5/30**, **P 4,0/30**. Secondary, one-lane: **P 4,0/20**, **P 3,5/20**. In notation, the figure is *free road width* in metres and denominator design speed km/h (presentation table; explicitly explained for forest categories but field category table is width/speed). | Table 1: “Doporučené návrhové kategorie polních cest.” | High for listed categories; medium for interpretation of width semantics absent accompanying norm text. |
| Designation and lane / crown | Categories distinguish main vs secondary and one- vs two-lane as above. The slide footnote says paved field tracks have shoulders **2 × 0.50 m** (justified cases 2 × 0.25 m), included in free road width. It does not give the separate carriageway width in this table. Complementary tracks have **no defined category**; locally designed width **3.0 m**, possibly **3.5 m**. Do not infer a standard carriageway width by subtracting shoulders: confirm the original norm/typical section. | Table 1 footnote, p. 11: “krajnice 2 × 0,50 m … započítává do volné šířky”; p. 11 text on doplňkové roads. | High for footnote and categories; low for an implied carriageway width (not supplied). |
| Passing places | The presentation/accessible text did not establish their spacing or dimensions; do not populate a game value from this evidence. | No attributable table/clause recovered. | Unverified. |
| Horizontal curves, paved | Table 2, p. 15: minimum radius **25 m at 30 km/h**, **12.5 m at 20 km/h** (footnote: lower value cannot be designed). Applies to paved field roads. | “Nejmenší poloměr oblouku Rdov … 25 … 12,5.” | High. |
| Transitions | Prefer simple circular curves; transitions only in justified cases, and only for main field roads. | p. 16: “oblouky s přechodnicemi … pouze v odůvodněných případech … u hlavních.” | High. |
| Horizontal widening | Paved field road widening is applied only for radii **<100 m**. One-lane widening uses Table 5 (p. 23), based on design vehicle; two-lane widening usually omitted. Table’s numeric rows were not recoverable from extracted text. | p. 21: “pouze u poloměrů menších než 100 m”; “u dvoupruhových … obvykle nenavrhuje.” | High on threshold/principle; unverified exact widening values. |
| Vertical curves | Table 4, p. 20: the table exists, but its numeric entries were not legible in extracted text. **No values asserted here.** | “Tabulka 4 – Nejmenší dovolené poloměry výškových oblouků zpevněných polních cest.” | Unverified numbers. |
| Longitudinal / resultant gradient, paved | Table 3, p. 18: at 30 km/h max longitudinal **15%**, resultant **16%**; at 20 km/h **18%**, resultant **19%**. Exceedance only justified, max **100 m**, with stated surfacing/traffic conditions. | Table 3; footnote: “v úseku délky max. 100 m.” | High. |
| Unpaved | Table 3 footnote: maximum longitudinal **10%**, resultant **11%**; above these, pave. | “Na nezpevněných … podélný sklon … 10 % a výsledný … 11 %.” | High. |

**Important correction to the prompt’s proposed gradient figures:** 15% / 18% are the paved limits for 30 / 20 km/h; the standard table also distinguishes resultant slope (16% / 19%). The 100 m exception in this slide is specifically tied to exceedance of paved maxima and additional conditions, not a blanket permission for any unpaved design.

## Forest roads — ČSN 73 6108 final draft (older text)

| Topic | Value / interpretation in final draft | Source, short Czech quote | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Classes / category families | **1L** = forest haulage road; **2L** = forest haulage road; **3L** = skidding road/svážnice (not equivalent to a public-access field track). Draft Table 1 (cl. 5.4, p. 14) recommends one-lane 1L categories **4.5/30, 4.5/20, 4.0/30, 4.0/20** and 2L categories **4.5/30, 4.5/20, 4.0/30, 4.0/20, 3.5/20**. Category numerator is *free width*, denominator speed. 3L has no design category. | Cl. 5.1–5.4, Table 1: “volnou šířku lesní cesty v m”; 3L “ani … 3L … se nenavrhují.” | High for draft. |
| Width / crown | Cl. 4.2 (printed p. 11): 1L recommended carriageway **3.5 m**, minimum **3.0 m**; recommended free width **4.5 m**, minimum **4.0 m**; must have passing places. 2L carriageway minimum **3.0 m**, free width minimum **3.5 m**, passing places required. 3L free width minimum **3.0 m**, no passing places. In this draft, road crown includes carriageway, shoulders and any passing areas (cl. 8.1). | Cl. 4.2: “šířka jízdního pruhu je 3,5 m (nejméně 3,0 m)”; “volná šířka … 4,5 m.” | High for draft. |
| Passing places | Cl. 8.7: for one-lane paved forest roads. Visibility from one place to the next required. Passing bay length **≥25 m**, total pavement width **6.50 m** (minimum **6.0 m**); can use junctions/accesses as passing opportunities. No spacing number prescribed in located clause. | Cl. 8.7.2–8.7.3, p. 22: “délky nejméně 25 m … celkové šířky 6,50 m (minimálně 6,0 m).” | High (draft); spacing unspecified. |
| Design speed | Cl. 7.2.2, p. 16: sealed pavement **30 km/h**, unsealed / operationally strengthened **20 km/h** for 1L/2L; 3L **15 km/h**. Category Table 1 restricts 30 km/h to sealed surfaces and 20 km/h to unsealed/operationally strengthened. | “se stmeleným krytem … 30 km/h”; “nestmeleným … 20 km/h.” | High for draft. |
| Horizontal curve minimum | Table 4, cl. 7.5.4, p. 17: paved forest roads **25 m at 30**, **15 m at 20**, **15 m at 15 km/h**. | “Nejmenší poloměr oblouku Rdov … 25, 15, 15.” | High for draft. |
| Transitions | No general transition-curve mandate found in draft; its curve treatment emphasizes circular arcs and crossfall change. Avoid asserting a universal “none” without full clause check. | Cl. 7.5 and 7.9. | Medium / qualified. |
| Vertical curves | Table 7, cl. 7.12.2, p. 19: sealed roads minimum crest and sag **200 m at 30**, **80 m at 20**, **50 m at 15 km/h**; minimum not specified for unpaved forest roads. | “Nejmenší dovolené poloměry Rv a Ru … 200 80 50”; “pro nezpevněné … nestanovuje.” | High for draft. |
| Gradients: paved | Table 6, cl. 7.10.3, p. 18: max longitudinal **10% at 30**, **12% at 20**; short-section exception, max length 100 m, subject to conditions. Table 5 (cl. 7.8, p. 17): max resultant **12% at 30**, **14% at 20**. | Table 6 “Největší dovolený podélný sklon”; Table 5 “Největší dovolený výsledný sklon.” | High for draft. |
| Gradients: unpaved / 3L | For unpaved 1L/2L: longitudinal maximum **10% on non-cohesive soils**, **8% on cohesive soils**; steeper sections must be hardened/drained, then max **16%** (cl. 7.10.3). Resultant maxima **12%** non-cohesive / **10%** cohesive, then hardened sections max **17%** (cl. 7.8.2). 3L: unpaved longitudinal max **20%**; hardened 3L maximum resultant **16%** (cl. 4.2). | Cl. 7.10.3, 7.8.2, 4.2. | High for draft. |

## Use in a game / remaining gaps

Treat OSM `highway=track` as a classification hint only: it does not identify whether the design basis is field road, forest haul road, skidding road, paved/unpaved, or the applicable category. Ask/select purpose and surface before applying limits. The 2013 field-track evidence supports listed categories and key maxima, but **does not provide verified separate carriageway widths, passing-place spacing/dimensions, numeric field vertical-curve radii, or exact widening values**. Retrieve the licensed ČSN 73 6109 text / SPÚ methodology before setting those parameters. Forest-road numbers here are from the final draft; verify against the 2018 published edition before labeling them current normative values.
