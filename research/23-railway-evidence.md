# Conventional railway evidence for M (single through track)

Research date: 2026-11-15. Scope: a narrow prototype for a new or relocated, standard-gauge, conventional single through track, passenger and freight mixed, design speeds no greater than 160 km/h. This is engineering-source reconnaissance, not a compliance specification. Values not directly evidenced below remain unknown/provisional; do not convert them into validity checks without further review.

## Governing sources and applicability

- **SŽDC S3 Part I, Change 4** (effective 1 March 2021, subsequent supplements shown in the public PDF; current public version retrieved): [PDF](https://www.spravazeleznic.cz/documents/50004227/139626480/SZDC_S3_Dil_01_sezm1az4_a_sopr1_20220101.pdf/08f34fa0-aabe-465a-8292-5c05e9a24cea?version=4.0). Articles 45–50 refer design of track geometry to ČSN 73 6360-1/-2; article 50 says spatial arrangement is governed by SŽ S11 “Prostorová průchodnost tratí” and ČSN 73 6320. This establishes the hierarchy, not the numerical tables. The PDF labels itself SŽDC; do not imply this is a newly revised 2026 SŽ document.
- **ČSN 73 6360-1:2013 + Z1**: [public preview](https://nahledy.normy.biz/n.php?i=511473) says it “stanoví požadavky na geometrické parametry koleje a kolejových spojení a rozvětvení z hlediska návrhu koleje železničních drah normálního rozchodu” (sets requirements for track geometry and connections/turnouts for normal-gauge railway design). Full tables are not available in the preview; numerical values below are not claimed as extracted from the current full standard.
- **SŽ S11 / ČSN 73 6320**: governing spatial-clearance references above. Public [ČSN 73 6320 preview](https://nahledy.normy.biz/nahled.php?i=21663) describes its scope as clearance profiles on standard-gauge national, regional and siding railways. A public SŽ presentation identifies SŽ S11 as effective 1 March 2021 and ČSN 73 6320 Z1; it is secondary evidence: [SUDOP presentation](https://www.sudop.cz/files/Cms:StaticPages:Blocks/17_Vrbata_S%C5%BD.pdf). Numeric profile/track-centre clearances remain **not verified** here. The 4.75 m track-centre spacing found in a project search result is project-specific, not a general norm.
- **TSI INF**: the familiar Commission Regulation (EU) No 1299/2014 [EUR-Lex text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2014/1299/oj/eng) is an older text and search results report it repealed from 1 March 2026 by Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/253. Therefore do not present 1299/2014 as the current TSI on this research date. The replacement’s current text and precise parameter tables were not independently retrieved/checked in this pass. TSI applicability is not universal to every retained track: older text explicitly applies to new, upgraded or renewed infrastructure and excludes existing infrastructure outside such works. For a relocation, identify the legal project scope and authorisation/TSI regime; do not treat an existing line as automatically subject to every new-build parameter. Check the official [EUR-Lex current regulation record/search for 2026/253](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/) before implementation.

### Cant formula: physics, not a design allowance

For standard gauge, equilibrium cant is approximately `D_eq(mm) = 11.8 × V(km/h)^2 / R(m)`. This is a physical relation (with rounding), not an allowed Czech cant, deficiency, excess, or minimum-radius rule. With mixed traffic, cant selected for one speed causes deficiency for faster traffic and excess for slower traffic; the permissible operational envelope and transition rates require the applicable current norm and vehicle approvals.

## Noise law and relocation

Current consolidated **NV 272/2011 Sb. §12(3), Annex 3 Part A**: [officially searchable Czech text](https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2011-272). The new-road/new-rail base is 50 dB plus a +10 dB correction for **new roads and railways** = **60 dB day**. At night, §12(3) applies an additional −10 dB generally, but explicitly −5 dB for rail traffic: **55 dB night**. These are hygienic limits for protected outdoor residential space, not rail-source emission inputs.

Applicability is project/legal-status dependent. The special base correction is phrased for a *new railway*; a relocation is physically new infrastructure but may be legally treated under the particular permit/alteration procedure and applicable transitional provisions. The regulation separately addresses existing infrastructure and certain maintenance/modernisation/upgrade cases; **this research did not establish that every relocation automatically receives 60/55**. Do not transfer the existing-line 55/50 case (or the new-line 60/55 case) solely from the word “relocation”; require legal/project classification. For a proposed route replacing an existing rail source, noise accounting must also replace—not double-count—the old operation.

## Cost evidence: what is and is not established

- SŽ says its public construction cost databases are hosted by SFDI: [SŽ cost-database page](https://www.spravazeleznic.cz/stavby-zakazky/modernizace/cenove-databaze), [SFDI OTSKP index](https://sfdi.gov.cz/cenove-databaze/otskp/). The SFDI page describes OTSKP as an industry classification/pricing system for construction, not a railway per-km tariff.
- Public SŽ tender documents commonly include priced “Soupis prací” and quantities; example indexed item-level contract amendment: [SŽ contract amendment, work schedule attachment](https://zakazky.spravazeleznic.cz/document_audit_179944/dodatek-c-1-k-sod-e650-s-5015_2020_21_02_2022_ke-zverejneni-pdf). Search metadata quotes line items for overhead contact-line wire supports, but this is not a complete railway unit-cost basis and the original priced schedule was not extracted/normalised here.
- No defensible current Czech unit prices were verified in this pass for track installation, ballast supply/placement, subgrade/formation, electrification, bridges, culverts or tunnels. Do **not** use road SPK category prices or international per-km averages as substitutes. To price: select recent SŽ procurement with public priced quantities, extract relevant line items and units from the awarded schedule, document year/VAT/scope/region/traffic staging, and separately model earthworks/structures/overhead-line systems. Contracts bundle enabling works, possessions, signalling, drainage and risk; a total divided by kilometres is not an item rate.

## Prototype boundary / follow-up required

A defensible first model can identify standard gauge, concrete sleepers, ballast track, and the 350 mm minimum ballast depth under a national/regional through track, while explicitly marking engineering checks as incomplete. Before enabling scored railway validity, retrieve and verify current ČSN 73 6360-1/-2 limits and transition equations, SŽ S4 formation rules, SŽ S11 and ČSN 73 6320 clearance profiles, current TSI INF 2026/253 applicability/text, and an operationally supported mixed-train gradient/vertical-curve envelope. Obtain comparable priced SŽ tender schedules. No stations or signalling assumptions are supported by this note.
