# 88 — A small town-bypass mission to replace Nová Paka: candidates and the pick (2026-11-19)

Why: the Nová Paka I/16 mission's terrain (ČÚZK DMR 5G) and aerial photo were captured while the real bypass was being
built: the real road's cuts are already in the ground (earthworks come cheap) and the photo shows the construction.
Wanted: a Czech class I town bypass, **planned and not started**, 4–9 km, with a published line (and ideally an OSM
`highway=proposed` line to draw as the planned line, as D3 / D35 have), a good story, and instructive terrain.

Method: four research subagents on the owner's examples, two more on OSM-found candidates; every key fact below was
re-read from the primary source (ŘSD leaflets) or measured here. OSM: all `highway=proposed` /
`highway=construction` ways with `proposed|construction=primary|trunk` in Czechia (Overpass,
maps.mail.ru mirror, 2026-11-19; the other public servers were overloaded), clustered by ref and place. Terrain:
DMR 5G sampled every 20 m along each OSM line (ČÚZK `dmr5g/ImageServer/exportImage` at 10 m). Traffic: ŘSD CSD 2025
(`geoportal.rsd.cz/.../ScitaniDopravy/MapServer/3`, `SV` all vehicles a day, `TV` heavy).

Terrain and photo cleanliness: ČÚZK's DMR 5G service describes the model as flown 2009–2013 ("v letech 2009 až
2013"), updated where the ground changed (Nová Paka's area was evidently re-flown during its works). None of the
shortlisted projects has started works beyond surveys and land purchase, so neither the terrain nor the current
Ortofoto ČR can contain them.

## The pick: I/46 Šternberk – obchvat

- **Small and fast:** 4.7 km, the shortest real candidate; a play area ≈ 6 × 8 km is ≈ 2 M cells at 5 m (Nová Paka's
  15 × 15 km is 9 M).
- **A story in one line:** 16,700 vehicles a day through the town under the castle; the bypass is ready but for a
  villa and butterflies.
- **Instructive terrain:** plain at the start, mountain at the end (the real project drops its design speed from 90 to
  70 km/h for it), one big valley (the 287 m viaduct), steep wooded slopes, allotments, a shooting range, a villa's
  garden: bridges vs embankments, a climb with a design-speed choice, houses to avoid.
- **A real line to compare:** the OSM proposed line (4.23 km, continuous, its bridges where the leaflet's are) and the
  leaflet's cost (2.04 bn) and structures.
- **Clean data:** no works have started (permit suspended until 2027 at the latest).

Runner-up: I/57 Valašské Meziříčí – Jarcová (busier town, bigger story, but a floodplain line of 2.7 km of bridges at
3.1 bn, and its category is half of a four-lane road).

## The mission as built (pack `i46-sternberk`, preset `i46-sternberk-road`, 2026-11-19)

- **Pack:** EPSG:5514 [-545500, -1113000, -538500, -1104500], 7.0 × 8.5 km at 5 m (1,400 × 1,700 = 2.38 M cells),
  ground 219–632 m, no DMR gaps; 6,964 buildings; 4.0 MB built (`pipeline/scenarios/i46-sternberk.json`). No
  protected area lies in it (AOPK: CHKO / EVL / PO Litovelské Pomoraví and PP Chomoutovské jezero only within
  2 km outside it). The streams the line crosses are not cut off by the map's edge (Sprchový potok 3.2 km² at the
  viaduct, Aleš 5.0 km²).
- **Planned line:** OSM ways 939310911 … 939310904 (13, `highway=proposed`, ref 46), 4,229 m, from node
  2929004274 on the existing I/46 south of the town to node 26401008 on the I/46 towards Opava. The leaflet's
  4,708 m includes the interchange split at the start (km 0.047) and the works at both ends.
- **Ends:** A and B are those two nodes, each declared a *merge* with the I/46 way it lies on (OSM 289348606 at A,
  4337741 at B; `ENDPOINT_JUNCTIONS.merge`, PROVISIONAL): our earthworks over it near the end are the junction's.
  Without the declaration Auto bridged the I/46 over our own end (62 M at A, 541 M at B). The real start is an
  interchange split (the old road becomes its branch); the game builds neither interchange.
- **Category and speed:** S 11.5 at 90 km/h, the real project's; its last three bends (past km 2.0) at 70 km/h, as the
  real project past its interchange at km 2.0.
- **Traffic: the town count, not a forecast.** No forecast for the bypass was found. The mission takes ŘSD CSD 2025 on
  the I/46 through Šternberk, the stretch the bypass replaces, from the town sign to the Domašov turn: sections
  7-1096 (16,731 vehicles a day, 1,299 heavy, 957 m), 7-1091 (12,816 / 707, 651 m), 7-2182 (9,671 / 866, 753 m) and
  7-2196 (5,241 / 723, 1,335 m), averaged over their lengths: **10,453 a day, 898 heavy (8.6 %)**. (The busiest
  section alone, 16,731, is over what a two-lane road carries at level of service C, 11,650, and would call for a
  four-lane road; the real project is a two-lane S 11.5 with climbing lanes, and much of the town's traffic is its
  own: the count falls to 5,241 on the road out to Opava.) The 8.6 % census heavy share is the noise model's heavy
  share.
- **Time target:** a mission choice, the mapped line + 10 % at the real project's speeds: 2.0 km at 90 and 2.23 km at
  70 → 3.57 → **3.6 min**.
- **Starter:** the mapped line's five curves as bends at its tangent intersections, radii near the mapped ones
  (R 1,500 / 1,000 / 800 / 360 / 340 m; the last two at the norm's limit for their spacing). VALID on its own, Auto
  grade. At A the I/46 forks off at 13° and stays under our slopes a few metres past the merge's 90 m zone; that
  mouth is now rebuilt with the junction (up to 30 m past the zone, as a side road's end rejoined to a rebuilt
  junction, `JUNCTION_TIE_IN`; footprint.ts). Before, the starter needed bend 1 moved 10 m, and 4 of 12 small drags of
  bend 1 broke on it (`scripts/small-drag-audit.ts --at 1000 --n 12 --radius 25`; now 0 of 12; km 2.5 and 3.5: 0 of 12).
  No other result of any pack changed (`scripts/exact.ts`, 186 of 191 identical, the 5 others this starter).

### Our bill of the starter against the ŘSD estimate (context, not a target)

Cost model 56, S 11.5, Auto grade (`scripts/scenarios/i46-sternberk/default.json`): **880 M CZK**, 4.24 km, 208 M/km,
3.2 min: road base 219, earthworks 56, bridges 178 (one 240 m viaduct over the Sprchový potok valley, km 3.89–4.13),
crossings 118 (2 tracks over us, 1 under, 6 moved, the two merges), other objects 159, buildings 84 (38, most of them
sheds of the allotments the real line goes through too), land 45, noise walls 18 (590 m), forest 3. The mapped line sampled every
1.5 km with Auto's radii (`reference.json`): 1,266 M (a 480 m bridge).

ŘSD's estimate is **2,037 M** (07/2025). What the real project builds that ours does not: two interchanges with
2,620 m of branches (the split at the start and Olomoucká street at km 2.056), six more bridges (520 m in all: the
branch crossing 83 m, the Aleš stream and the chalet road 56 m, a ravine 42 m, …), climbing lanes, 1,560 m of noise
walls, 1,186 m of retaining walls, temporary connections at both ends and 85 objects in all (utilities, field
roads). Our line keeps close to the ground (cuts up to 6.6 m, fills up to 8.1 m, 11 m at the viaduct's end) and fills the ravine at km
3.2 that the real one bridges. So ours is −57 % on a project with much more in it; the card shows only our bill.
