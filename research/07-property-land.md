# Property and land costs — Trutnov / Czechia

**Scope:** new class I S 9.5 road near Trutnov; nominal 2025/26 CZK, excl. VAT unless noted. Web sources checked 2026-11-14. Listed estate prices are asking prices and can include land; they are not clean completed-sale evidence. Confidence means confidence in using the recommended game input, not in parcel-specific appraisal.

## 1. Liniový zákon §3b: what the multipliers multiply

Current consolidated wording consulted at [Zákony pro lidi, Act 416/2009 §3b](https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2009-416), checked 2026-11-14 (also [e-Sbírka, version from 2023-07-01](https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2009/416/2023-07-01?odkazId=63696093&predmetneDatum=2024-06-30), checked 2026-11-14):

> „(1) Získávají-li se potřebná práva podle § 3a koupí, sjednává se v kupní smlouvě kupní cena ve výši a) ceny stanovené znaleckým posudkem vynásobené koeficientem 8 v případě pozemku s výjimkou stavebního pozemku, nebo b) ceny stanovené znaleckým posudkem vynásobené koeficientem 1,5 v případě stavebního pozemku nebo stavby.“
>
> „(2) Znalecký posudek … stanoví cenu ve výši obvyklé ceny pozemku nebo stavby, včetně všech jejich součástí a příslušenství. Nelze-li stanovit cenu ve výši obvyklé ceny pozemku nebo stavby, stanoví znalecký posudek cenu ve výši zjištěné ceny pozemku nebo stavby. V takovém případě obsahuje znalecký posudek zdůvodnění nemožnosti stanovení ceny ve výši obvyklé ceny…“

Thus **8× is not specifically a multiplier of “official/decree price”**. It multiplies the appraised amount: normally *cena obvyklá* (market usual price), and only where that cannot be determined, a reasoned *cena zjištěná* under valuation rules. Same base-price fallback applies to the 1.5× category. Non-building parcels (agricultural, forest, etc.) → 8×; building land and structures → 1.5×. Statute also says valuation reflects actual condition on valuation date and disregards value changes connected with planned infrastructure; its subsection 4 describes when appraised price is treated as usual price for expropriation admissibility. **Important:** only qualifying voluntary acquisitions under §§3a–3b; it is not an automatic multiplier for every land transaction or the expropriation award. High confidence.

## 4. Forest clearance and PUPFL levy

[Forest Act 289/1995 §17 and annex](https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/1995-289), checked 2026-11-14: permanent or temporary removal from PUPFL incurs a levy. Annex calculation is based on affected area, forest production/price inputs and function coefficient; temporary removal is an annual levy, permanent removal is a one-off amount (statutory annex gives the formula). The detailed result is site-specific—forest category/function, stand and area—not a flat charge per m². Applications/details are governed by [Decree 77/1996](https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/1996-77). A reliable current Trutnov spruce/mixed-forest example and current coefficient worksheet could not be verified: **UNVERIFIED**. Do not present the placeholder **250 CZK/m²** as a statutory levy. Recommend **0–50 CZK/m²** provisional levy (midpoint 20; low confidence) pending the forestry authority's calculation; retain timber value/compensation separately.

Clearing: SFDI's 2026 OTSKP item 11201 (tree felling incl. stump, trunk ≤0.5 m) is reported at **1,589 CZK/tree**, [SFDI OTSKP database](https://sfdi.gov.cz/cenove-databaze/otskp/), 2026 release, as recorded in [research/00-feasibility-map.md §3.2](00-feasibility-map.md); checked 2026-11-14. This is not a forest-clearance rate per area and may not include timber processing/haul/disposal. Recommend **15–40 CZK/m²** stand-clearance allowance, range **5–80**, low confidence; price by stems/size and salvage value in real estimates. Thus +250 CZK/m² is likely a large overstatement for ordinary clearing/levy alone (but can conflate purchase compensation, mature timber and clearing).

## 5. Demolition (including waste/disposal)

Czech cost-system description prices building demolition by **m³ of enclosed volume**, not floor area ([Cenová soustava, demolition section 800-6](https://www.cenovasoustava.cz/default.asp?Typ=1&ID=1&BId=1&Pop=1&IDmH=1731304&IDm=1083127&Menu=HSV), checked 2026-11-14). Published contractor starting prices include **1,000 CZK/m³** for a house (Demolice Plzeň, [price page](https://demoliceplzen.cz/sluzba/demolice-cena/), checked 2026-11-14); actual disposal, asbestos, sorting, access, foundations and permits alter totals. With typical 2.5–3 m storey height this converts roughly to **2,500–3,000 CZK/m² floor** at the advertised starting rate. Recommend **3,000 CZK/m² floor**, range **2,000–5,000** for houses, **3,000–8,000** for dense/multi-storey blocks, **1,000–4,000** for simple halls; confidence medium-low. Keep **3,000** placeholder for ordinary house only; a single uniform rate misses height, materials and hazardous waste. All are rough, not verified tender averages.

## 6. Effects outside the footprint: buyouts, protection zone, severance

- **Silniční ochranné pásmo is not a standard house-buyout radius.** Act 13/1997 §30 ([current law](https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/1997-13), checked 2026-11-14) sets for a newly built/reconstructed class I road outside built-up area a zone typically **50 m from the road axis** (special cases vary); it regulates construction/activities and requires approvals, it does not make existing houses automatically illegal or mandate purchase. [Ministry of Transport explanation](https://md.gov.cz/Zivotni-situace/Pozemni-komunikace/Ochranne-pasmo), 24 Sep 2020.
- **Noise is assessed, not solved by a universal distance rule.** New road design uses traffic/noise modelling, barriers, alignment and sometimes façade measures; buyout may be considered where legal limits cannot otherwise be met or a home becomes unusable, but no general statutory threshold in metres or confirmed typical ŘSD buyout distance was found. **UNVERIFIED:** representative counts and costs of noise-driven home buyouts on D35/D11/obchvat projects. Recent D1 Přerov–Říkovice reporting concerns residents/extra noise measures rather than verified house-buyout count: [Přerov municipality, 2016 acquisition for a bridge pier in Dluhonice](https://www.prerov.eu/cs/magistrat/tiskove-centrum/tiskove-zpravy-2016/tz-prosinec-2016/misto-domu-tu-budou-stat-pilire-pro-dalnici-klicova-smlouva-na-odkup-uz-byla-podepsana.html), 12 Dec 2016; [ČT24 on post-opening additional noise treatment](https://ct24.ceskatelevize.cz/clanek/regiony/hluk-z-dalnicniho-mostu-u-dluhonic-zmirni-specialni-kryt-379061), 27 Oct 2026. Not evidence of an ordinary setback buyout radius.
- **Access severed / plots cut:** exact project practice and policy not confirmed. The statutory power to acquire necessary rights does not imply automatic acquisition of every remnant. Where remainder is unusable, owners may seek extension of expropriation to the residual property under [Expropriation Act 184/2006 §4](https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2006-184) (see [MMR explanation](https://mmr.gov.cz/cs/ministerstvo/stavebni-pravo/pravo-a-legislativa/zakon-o-vyvlastneni), checked 2026-11-14); negotiated whole-parcel purchase may occur. **UNVERIFIED:** whether ŘSD typically buys an entire garden/yard whenever the footprint divides it. Game rule should buy required footprint, add a modest probability/penalty for unusable remnants, and not assume whole-parcel purchase by default.
- Recommend no fixed-distance home-acquisition cost. If a strategic simplified model needs an option, flag houses only where a noise/access feasibility check fails; use full property replacement/acquisition cost plus relocation/demolition, not blanket buffers. Confidence high that 50 m is a regulatory zone, low on any buyout frequency/distance.

## 7. Expropriation, time and relocation

[Expropriation Act 184/2006](https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2006-184), [government service on compensation](http://portal.gov.cz/sluzby-vs/nahrady-pri-vyvlastneni-S7627), and [MMR overview](https://mmr.gov.cz/cs/ministerstvo/stavebni-pravo/pravo-a-legislativa/zakon-o-vyvlastneni), checked 2026-11-14: compensation is generally the usual value of property/rights, not the §3b voluntary-purchase 8×/1.5× incentive. Equivalent replacement property can be agreed. I did not verify a generally applicable statutory lump-sum relocation bonus or a reliable Czech average extra case duration/cost; **UNVERIFIED**. Expropriation requires prior attempt at agreement and a separate administrative/judicial process; project-level schedule delay is highly case-specific. Model as a risk contingency/time penalty rather than a false universal cost, e.g. **6–24 months delay** (recommendation only; low confidence, not sourced average) and case/legal/appraisal expense **UNVERIFIED**. Actual proven consequential losses may require separate assessment; do not assume all moving costs are automatically paid as a standard bonus.
