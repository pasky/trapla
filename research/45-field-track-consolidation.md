# Field-track consolidation at Czech motorways

**Research note (15 Nov 2026).** “Field-track crossing” here means a motorway structure carrying a public/agricultural route, not a wildlife-only crossing. Confidence describes confidence in the reported source/fact, not universal applicability. I found no Czech rule setting a maximum crossing interval, detour, or parallel-track length; do not treat the game suggestions below as statutory values.

## 1. How routes are selected / spacing and detours

- **The documented Czech logic is network/access-based, not a fixed interval rule.** SPÚ’s *Metodický návod pro provádění pozemkových úprav* says the shared-facilities plan includes new/reconstructed field roads and associated bridges/culverts. The network must “zajistit přístup na pozemky”, connect relevant farms/municipalities, keep the landscape permeable and link to existing roads and neighboring cadastral areas. It also says to use existing roads first where compatible with transport/erosion/parcel-layout needs, and that road category is an expert decision based on traffic and farm machinery. **No maximum crossing spacing or acceptable detour is stated** in the passages on the network.
  - Source: [SPÚ, MN 01/2022, sections 13 and 13.1, pp. 94–97](https://spu.gov.cz/frontend/webroot/uploads/files/2024/02/mn01_2022-metodickynavodproprovadenipozemkovychupravzmenac13746.213746.213746.pdf). **Confidence: high** (primary national methodology; conclusion is limited to its relevant provisions).
- **A motorway project may lead to rerouting/shortening/connection rather than rebuilding each severed track as an overbridge.** In the D35 Janov–Opatovec KoPÚ procurement, several affected paths are shortened or tied into an eastern/western motorway service road, or connected to another path/road; e.g. DO 1 at km 62.4 is shortened and connected to the eastern accompanying road, while DO 40 at the same km connects to DO 1. This documents the sort of consolidation described in the question, but does not establish a general design threshold.
  - Source: [SPÚ procurement, KoPÚ D35 Svitavy, description of D35 study (Agroplan 2021), pp. 4–6](https://zakazky.spucr.cz/document_242055/b20a590138d0facd3a0acfdeae7be092-zadavaci-dokumentace-kopu-d35-sy_podpis-pdf). Quote: “navrženo zkrácení a napojení na východní doprovodnou komunikaci dálnice D35.” **Confidence: high** for this specific project.
- **No Czech numeric customary maximum found** for (a) motorway agricultural-crossing spacing (including 1–2 km), (b) agricultural detour/route extension, or (c) maximum length of a parallel access road. The SPÚ methodology does say supplementary field paths should be exceptional and their “délka by měla být minimalizována”; this is qualitative, not a length limit. It separately gives *optimal catchment areas* of 100 ha for a primary and 50 ha for a secondary field road (and infers up to 10 ha for supplementary paths). These are not motorway-crossing intervals.
  - Source: same SPÚ MN, pp. 96–97. **Confidence: high** for wording and categories; **high** that no numeric cap is in these sections, not proof no project-specific rule exists elsewhere.
- **International backup (not Czech law/practice): UNVERIFIED.** No RLW/German, Austrian or Dutch numeric threshold was verified in this research. Do not import a foreign value as a Czech norm.

## 2. Project examples and counts

- **D35 Janov–Opatovec / Svitavy KoPÚ:** The SPÚ document describes *four* KoPÚ in territory affected by D35 and names multiple affected paths and their proposed adjustments. At km 62.4 two paths (DO 1, DO 40) are served by a shared connection arrangement (DO 40 to DO 1; DO 1 to the eastern service road). At km 62.5 two others receive separate endpoint adjustments (DO 45 to I/35; DO 2 to the western service road). For Mikuleč, C14 (km 69.5) connects to the northern service road; C12/C18 (km 69.4) connect to III/03532. **This is evidence of network consolidation/rerouting, not a verified count of severed tracks versus crossing structures.**
  - Source/quotes: [SPÚ KoPÚ D35 tender](https://zakazky.spucr.cz/document_242055/b20a590138d0facd3a0acfdeae7be092-zadavaci-dokumentace-kopu-d35-sy_podpis-pdf), pp. 3–6. **Confidence: high** for described connections; **low** for inferring a count of consolidated tracks because the document does not label a one-to-one “track crossed / structure built” comparison.
- **D11, Chýšť–Osičky, project leaflet:** ŘSD reports “přeložka polních cest: 5” for the named section. This is five field-road diversions, **not** five overbridges and not a count of old tracks consolidated at a single structure. The leaflet’s same project summary separately reports highway bridges and overpasses, without a breakdown tying field roads to individual structures.
  - Source: [ŘSD, Dálnice D11 leaflet](https://rsd.cz/documents/38144/80614/Publikace-D11-08-2017-dvoustrany.pdf), project 1104/1A. **Confidence: high** for the reported five diversions; **low** as evidence of track-to-structure consolidation.
- **Requested matched statistics (“X km, Y polní-road bridges” and number of tracks joined): UNVERIFIED.** The accessible project summaries found here count relocations, not pre-project field-track crossings versus built crossing objects. Avoid claiming a typical ratio.

## 3. Costs, land consolidation and replacement-road standards

- **Cost responsibility is shared/decided case-by-case, not simply “KoPÚ pays” or “motorway pays.”** SPÚ’s methodology says for land adjustments triggered by construction (especially motorways), a study may define the area affected and “podíl na nákladech PÚ” of the construction investor; if only a small area is affected, e.g. “jedna přístupová cesta hrazená stavebníkem”, the study may be unnecessary. This explicitly allows a construction-funded access road but does not say every replacement path is always paid by the motorway budget.
  - Source: [SPÚ MN 01/2022, “Zahájení řízení v důsledku stavební činnosti”, p. 37](https://spu.gov.cz/frontend/webroot/uploads/files/2024/02/mn01_2022-metodickynavodproprovadenipozemkovychupravzmenac13746.213746.213746.pdf). **Confidence: high** for principle; individual allocation remains project-specific.
- **D35 example shows KoPÚ and motorway coordination:** the SPÚ tender commissions four KoPÚ in the D35 footprint; says these address territory directly affected by construction. It records the ŘSD position that it wants the motorway on ŘSD land after construction and KoPÚ. This is evidence of coordination, not by itself proof that SPÚ rather than ŘSD pays for every road. The tender’s study lists new access roads in Mikuleč, including C1 0.754 km, C18 1.234 km, C12 0.367 km, C13 0.908 km and others; these are access-road lengths, not a maximum permitted length.
  - Source: [SPÚ KoPÚ D35 tender](https://zakazky.spucr.cz/document_242055/b20a590138d0facd3a0acfdeae7be092-zadavaci-dokumentace-kopu-d35-sy_podpis-pdf), pp. 3, 5–8. Quote: “Potřeba dalších cest ke zpřístupnění pozemků vyplyne z návrhu nového uspořádání pozemků.” **Confidence: high** for example and wording.
- **SPÚ’s MN gives recommended replacement field-road categories (referring to ČSN 73 6109):** primary roads one-lane P 4.5/30 or P 4.0/30, two-lane P 6.0/30; secondary roads P 4.0/20 or P 3.5/20. For paved roads, shoulders are normally 2 × 0.50 m (in justified cases 2 × 0.25 m), included in the free width. Category is chosen by professional judgment and machinery/traffic requirements. Supplementary roads have no defined design category, are generally unpaved and at least 3.0 m (or 3.5 m as appropriate); use only exceptionally and keep short. **P 4.0/30 is not an across-the-board substitute-track rule.**
  - Source: [SPÚ MN 01/2022, pp. 95–97](https://spu.gov.cz/frontend/webroot/uploads/files/2024/02/mn01_2022-metodickynavodproprovadenipozemkovychupravzmenac13746.213746.213746.pdf). **Confidence: high** (official methodology reproducing recommendations; detailed design still depends on current standard and context).

## Recommended game rules (simulation, not Czech legal requirements)

1. **Do not enforce a hard crossing interval or detour cap as a Czech norm.** Select crossings/routes by access to farm parcels, connectivity to the existing network and roads on both sides, terrain, parcel pattern, environmental/water constraints, and cost. **Confidence: high** that the inspected Czech guidance is qualitative rather than a numeric spacing/detour rule.
2. **Permit consolidation** when several low-volume tracks can be linked along a short service/parallel road to a neighboring crossing and all resulting parcels remain accessible. Provide a player-facing reason (“joined to the nearby crossing; this avoids another bridge”) and show the added route distance. A quantitative maximum detour should be a clearly labelled **provisional game threshold**, not attributed to ČSN/SPÚ. **Confidence: medium** (consistent with documented D35 reroutes; threshold itself unverified).
3. **Cost the replacement route as a project-specific choice:** motorway project, KoPÚ/shared-facilities works, or an agreed split. Do not charge KoPÚ automatically; for major motorway-triggered KoPÚ, represent a negotiated investor contribution. **Confidence: high** on case-by-case principle.
4. **Use SPÚ category guidance for cost/footprint defaults:** secondary P 3.5/20 or P 4.0/20; primary P 4.0/30 or P 4.5/30; supplementary unpaved ≥3.0 m and short. Select class by use/machinery, not solely by whether it replaces a crossing. **Confidence: high**.

## Adopted in the game (cost model 28)

- No Czech maximum detour or crossing spacing was found, so none is imposed: Auto leads a cut track or
  service road to a neighbouring crossing (or a structure and the roads led to it to a neighbour within the
  existing 750 m shortlist) wherever that is cheaper and buildable — the D35 KoPÚ practice of shortening a field
  road and joining it to a neighbouring one or to the motorway's accompanying road.
- A crossed road that runs into the motorway's earthworks and back out on the same side is moved beside them as
  an accompanying road (*doprovodná komunikace*), priced as new road of its class.
- Replacement pieces are priced at the crossed road's own SPK class (tracks: paved P4, as before), not at a
  separate KoPÚ standard; who pays (motorway or land consolidation) is not modelled.
