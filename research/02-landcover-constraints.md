# Land cover, land use and surface constraints — feasibility inventory

Scope: public geospatial inputs relevant to surface clearance, acquisition, avoidance and exposure. “Open” below means a usable public download/service; verify the dataset’s own terms before bundling derived data. **UNVERIFIED** marks claims not confirmed against a current primary metadata/license page. Checked source pages: ČÚZK open-data catalogue, ESA WorldCover data access, MZe LPIS and VÚMOP BPEJ pages; links below are the authoritative starting points.

## 1. Land cover / land use

| Source | Content / scale | Format, access, reuse |
|---|---|---|
| **OpenStreetMap (OSM)** | Tagged polygons/lines: `landuse=*` (farmland, residential, forest, industrial), `natural=*` (wood, water, grassland, wetland), plus buildings/roads/rail. Czech coverage is generally rich around settlement/transport; not a consistent remotely sensed classification, and rural boundaries/attributes are variable. | Planet or regional extracts (PBF), Overpass; free. ODbL: attribution and share-alike obligations for derivative databases; tile/render terms differ. No official completeness metric. Download from https://download.geofabrik.de/europe/czech-republic.html ; tagging guide https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Map_features |
| **ESA WorldCover** | Global 11-class land cover, 10 m, 2020 (v100) & 2021 (v200); class map, not cadastral/use-purpose truth. Global map stated ~117 GB. | GeoTIFF/COG tiles, WMS/WMTS; public S3 no-sign-request, no account for direct download. CC BY 4.0, free including commercial use with attribution. Important gotcha: different algorithm versions mean 2020–21 differences are not purely real change. https://esa-worldcover.org/en/data-access |
| **Copernicus CORINE Land Cover (CLC)** | Europe-wide 44 classes, 100 m raster and vector polygons; minimum mapping unit 25 ha, minimum width 100 m; 6-year epochs. Too coarse for corridor-level obstacles, useful for regional land-use baseline. | Download via Copernicus Land Monitoring Service; free under Copernicus data terms (reuse incl. commercial with acknowledgement). https://land.copernicus.eu/en/products/corine-land-cover ; specific current download endpoints **UNVERIFIED**. |
| **CLC+ Backbone** | Pan-European 10 m raster land-cover baseline, latest product page lists 2023; complements CLC, less land-use detail than CLC classes. | Copernicus download, raster; free under Copernicus terms, attribution. Product page verified; download may require the Copernicus Data Space registration **UNVERIFIED**. https://land.copernicus.eu/en/products/clc-backbone |
| **Copernicus HRL** | Tree Cover Density (percent canopy), forest type, imperviousness (sealed surface); pan-European, generally 10 m raster for recent vintages. Distinguishes canopy/sealing better than broad classes; not legal parcel status. | Copernicus download, GeoTIFF; free terms. Exact current versions and direct/no-login flow **UNVERIFIED**. https://land.copernicus.eu/en/products/high-resolution-layer-forests-and-tree-cover ; https://land.copernicus.eu/en/products/high-resolution-layer-imperviousness |
| **Urban Atlas / Dynamic World** | Urban Atlas: detailed land-use/land-cover polygons for EU functional urban areas, MMU 0.25 ha urban/1 ha rural (reference years). Dynamic World: global 10 m, near-real-time class probabilities from Sentinel-2 (2015 onward). | Urban Atlas downloadable vector/raster via Copernicus; free Copernicus terms. | **Overture Maps** | Global vector buildings, places, transportation, divisions; not a comprehensive land-cover classification (land-use/theme coverage is not a substitute for imagery products). | Bulk GeoParquet releases on S3/Overture, free. Overture data under CDLA Permissive 2.0; source-specific attribution/third-party terms can apply. https://docs.overturemaps.org/ ; https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution/ |

## 2. Czech national sources and parcels

| Source | Content / scale | Format, access, reuse |
|---|---|---|
| **ČÚZK ZABAGED** | National vector topographic model: settlements/buildings, roads, rail, hydrography, vegetation, utility/distribution networks and product pipelines, protected areas, relief; contour/elevation products separately. Strong practical base for surface features. | Full planimetric data are now published as open data, CC BY 4.0; web download, WFS/ATOM. ČÚZK says open-data outputs include state-wide and local/municipal variants; no fee/login indication on catalogue. https://geoportal.cuzk.gov.cz/Default.aspx?lng=EN&mode=TextMeta&side=data_uvod&text=data_sady&head_tab=sekce-05-gp&menu=52 ; metadata https://geoportal.cuzk.cz/Default.aspx?lng=EN&mode=TextMeta&text=dSady_zabaged&side=zabaged&menu=24 |
| **Data50 / Data200** | Generalized national geographic models; Data50 nominal 1:50,000, Data200 1:200,000. Useful small-scale context, not design-grade corridor detail. | ČÚZK lists Data50 and Data250 as open, not Data200 on the page inspected; Data200 availability **UNVERIFIED** (possibly legacy product). Data50 open download. Same catalogue URL above. |
| **RÚIAN** | Address/building units, building footprints/identifiers, municipalities/cadastral areas and land-use/land-type registry attributes. It is a registry, not uniformly surveyed physical surface; parcels are better obtained from cadastral-map files. | VFR nationwide/monthly plus daily changes; CSV/SHP variants, country or municipality; open data. | **Cadastral map (KM)** | Parcel boundaries and map geometry by cadastral territory; cadastral land type is registered attribute, not necessarily observed cover. | ČÚZK confirms open data: VFK, plus reduced SHP/DXF/DGN; monthly VFK, SHP weekly. | **LPIS (MZe/SZIF)** | Farmer-use blocks, crop declarations, vineyards and landscape features; agricultural use, not legal parcel ownership. | **BPEJ / agricultural soil** | Five-digit soil ecological quality units: climate, soil unit, slope/exposure, depth/skeletal characteristics; mapping polygons and unit quality classes. | **ČÚZK orthophoto** | Aerial image, 0.20 m pixel (current Czech orthophoto specification; update split-area cycle). Great visual validation, not automatically extractable land-use labels. | ČÚZK confirms current orthophoto data are open; WMS/WMTS and map sheets/download. ## 4. Buildings, population, transport, costs

| Data | Findings |
|---|---|
| **Building footprints** | RÚIAN is the best authoritative Czech starting point, with open ČÚZK downloads (above). OSM buildings add mapping detail but ODbL. Overture buildings globally as GeoParquet, source attribution obligations: https://docs.overturemaps.org/guides/buildings/ . Microsoft footprints are available as open building datasets but country-specific coverage/terms **UNVERIFIED**: https://github.com/microsoft/GlobalMLBuildingFootprints . |
| **Population** | CZSO 2021 census 1 km grid is publicly catalogued; useful for coarse noise exposure, much too coarse for individual house impacts. Download format/license endpoints **UNVERIFIED**. https://data.gov.cz/dataset?iri=https%3A%2F%2Fdata.gov.cz%2Fzdroj%2Fdatov%C3%A9-sady%2F00025593%2F33a742e51c1ac89c1a17008084e2412e . EU GEOSTAT / Eurostat grids 1 km and GHSL (global, 100 m/1 km products by epoch) generalize beyond CZ; GHSL open under CC BY 4.0. https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/gisco/geodata/population-distribution-demography/geostat ; https://human-settlement.emergency.copernicus.eu/ |
| **Existing roads/rail** | OSM is easily available and typically detailed in CZ, but tagging/completeness are not guaranteed. Underlying segment-level GIS AADT download and reuse terms **UNVERIFIED**; UNECE offers Czech 2020 XLS and a network map, not necessarily downloadable GIS segments. https://opendata.rsd.cz/ ; https://scitani.rsd.cz/CSD_2020/pages/map/default.aspx ; https://unece.org/transport/statistics-transport/traffic-census-2020 . ## Feasibility takeaways (data only)

Strong Czech foundation: open **ZABAGED + RÚIAN/cadastral map + orthophoto**, augmented by OSM and 10 m WorldCover/CLC+ for broad cover. ZABAGED’s newly open CC BY 4.0 status is especially consequential. BPEJ/LPIS offer unusually relevant agricultural detail, but confirm redistribution terms and distinguish registered agricultural use from observed cover. For actual engineering-cost constraints, legal protected/flood/mining layers and detailed utility/rail data have uneven access; treat availability as layer-specific rather than assume one open national database. 1 km population grids and 100 m CLC are screening-scale only. WorldCover’s freely downloadable tiles are substantial but manageable as regional subsets; CLC+ 10 m is the stronger Europe-wide land-cover counterpart. Exact service download probes beyond the described public catalogue pages were not performed.
