# 106 — Taking up an old road bridge

## Findings

- **SFDI / SPOŽES:** The public SFDI index describes SPOŽES as a railway-study cost collection. I did not locate a public **L3_Mosty / H06 “demolice stávajícího mostu”** road-bridge norm or a verifiable CZK/m² value. The 2023 SPOŽES workbook contains H06 “Železniční most - demolice” (railway bridge demolition), unit m²; this is not the requested road-bridge item and its row does not give a directly usable CZK/m² price. [SFDI SPOŽES index](https://sfdi.gov.cz/cenove-databaze/spozes/); [2023 SPOŽES workbook](https://sfdi.gov.cz/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/2023-spozes.xlsx), sheet “SPOŽES2023 p.č.2 Soubor položek”, row 143.

- **OTSKP priced item (older reference):** **4,720 Kč/m³**, year/date not stated on the price sheet (PDF metadata 2009), item 96616, “BOURÁNÍ KONSTRUKCÍ ZE ŽELEZOBETONU”; text below says landfill fees without/with fees. [OTSKP-SPK SSD9 price sheet](http://tridniky.cz/PDF/SSD9_cena.pdf), row 96616. This is a generic concrete-demolition norm, not a bridge-specific rate; its age makes it unsuitable as the sole current estimate.

- **Recent OTSKP-priced example:** **2,600 Kč/m³**, OTSKP ~2024, item 966168 “BOURÁNÍ KONSTRUKCÍ ZE ŽELEZOBETONU S ODVOZEM DO 20KM.” The same row says it includes handling and transport to landfill; landfill fee is listed separately under 0141**. This is a construction-budget instance (for a small catchpit, not a bridge), not a universal expert rate. [Signed contract / priced bill, item 59](https://profilzadavatele-vz.cz/download_file/file/303b7fuug0ldhontovsvkl0ct5_1777029691_smlouva%20o%20d%C3%ADlo%20komplet%20-%20chodn%C3%ADk.pdf), PDF p. 50 (printed budget row).

- **Bridge geometry for conversion:** The 2016 OTSKP take-off for II/101 Zápy bridge 101-074b separately quantities **955.86 m³** of reinforced-concrete bridge demolition and describes a **16 m × 77 m** bridge (deck area 1,232 m²): **0.776 m³ reinforced concrete per m²**. The document is an unpriced tender take-off; it supplies geometry/quantity, not a demolition price. It also quantities plain-concrete demolition separately. [II/101 Zápy, unpriced bill](https://zakazky.kr-stredocesky.cz/document_5857/8bf504a2dfd94d3fb000bf49719aaa9b-zapy_vv-pdf), “SO 001 Demolice stávajícího mostu”, item 966168 and quantity take-off; 20 July 2017.

- **German fallback:** I did not find the primary source for the quoted ≈€330/m² small bridge / ≈€500/m² valley bridge figures, so I do not use them.
- **Abandoned road tunnel portal sealing:** Not researched to a citable cost in this pass.

## Recommended game input

**2,000 Kč/m² deck area** for demolition of a typical small concrete road bridge (study-level, direct structural demolition and haul; disposal fees/exceptional traffic staging excluded). Basis: the Zápy bridge take-off gives 0.776 m³/m²; applying the recent OTSKP-priced 2,600 Kč/m³ example gives about **2,018 Kč/m²**, rounded to 2,000. This is a transparent proxy, not a published bridge-specific norm.
