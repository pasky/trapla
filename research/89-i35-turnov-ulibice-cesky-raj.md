# 89 — The "D35 through Český ráj": I/35 Turnov – Úlibice (2026-11-19)

Why: the owner asked for a new scenario, "the D35 through Český ráj", the planned section Turnov – Úlibice
(Jičín) through or around the Bohemian Paradise, famous for its variant fight over CHKO Český ráj. This note
sources the project as it stands for the mission design. Method: four research subagents (project and status;
engineering parameters; protected areas; the variant names), then every key fact below re-read here from the
primary source (ŘSD portal and press releases, the 2021 EIA opinion, the 2020 EIA documentation, the 2023 ŘSD
technical study and its cost estimate, all opened 2026-11-19); the overlay of the OSM line on the AOPK layers and
the ŘSD census were computed here.

## 1. What it is: not a motorway any more, the class I road I/35

- **Not D35.** The line was the R35 expressway until 2015; from 1 Jan 2016 (the R category abolished, zákon
  268/2015) R35 Úlibice – Lipník became D35, while Turnov – Úlibice was "vyřazena z plánované dálniční sítě a
  zařazena mezi silnice pro motorová vozidla" [WD35]. ŘSD's project is **"Stavba silnice I/35 Turnov – Úlibice"**
  [P], "přeložka silnice I/35" [N26]; the EIA calls it a "silnice I. třídy" [S21]. The public and the opponents
  still call it "D35" [SOS].
- **Why that matters here:** zákon 114/1992 §26(1)(f) bans new *motorways* anywhere in a CHKO (research/75); a
  class I road needs the authority's consent (§44) in zones III–IV. ŘSD: "Trasujeme kapacitní komunikaci mimo
  území Českého ráje" [LK25]. (On the AOPK layer the mapped line crosses a corner of zone III, §3.)
- **Variant names** [S21] p. 1: "Trasa je navržena v základní variantě E1 vedené severovýchodním obchvatem
  Rovenska pod Troskami a variantě E2, která je vedena jihozápadním obchvatem Rovenska pod Troskami. Varianta E1
  je navržena ve dvou podvariantách" — **E11** all 2+1 (S 15.25/110), **E12** four-lane S 21.5/110 from MÚK
  Ohrazenice to MÚK Žernov, then 2+1 S 15.25/110. "Celková délka varianty E1 je 32,5 km … varianty E2 je 31,5 km."
  The opinion approved **E1 / E12** ("Záměr … ve variantě E1, podvariantě E12"). The 2023 study: "Trasa je
  navržena v jedné stabilizované směrové variantě (ve zjišťovacím řízení původně označené E1)" [TST]. (ŘSD's portal
  text calls it "Varianta E2 … 31,5 km" [P]: that conflicts with the EIA's naming; the study and the opinion are the
  primary documents, E1/E12 is the line.)
- **The old fight** (the southern variant): MŽP backed a southern route round the CHKO along the I/16 and II/268
  (Jičín – Sobotka – Mnichovo Hradiště, ≈ 15 bn vs ≈ 20 bn for the northern one at 2008 prices); the Liberec
  regional assembly chose the northern one on 30 Mar 2010, against the CHKO administration and the S.O.S. Český ráj
  coalition [WD35, iDNES 2010]. In Aug 2026 the coalition again asked for the southern variant [SOS]; ŘSD: "S jižní
  variantou se nepočítá. Ta neřeší napojení Semilska … není zakotvena ve vyšších územně-plánovacích dokumentech"
  [N26].

## 4. The reference line in OSM

OSM ways **1012219137** (3,487 m, from the D10/I/35 at Ohrazenice, node at 50.59751 N 15.11828 E), **1012219136**
("Tunel Pelešany", 926 m, layer −1) and **883368325** (28,606 m, name "D35", to 50.44542 N 15.43819 E where the
D35 Úlibice bypass, mapped `highway=construction`, starts): all `highway=proposed`, `proposed=trunk`, ref 35;
**33.02 km** in all, as the study's 33.0 km, its tunnel the study's 900 m. It passes north-east of Rovensko (the
E1 line). Its distances to the small protected areas match the 2018 dendrological survey's E1 figures [D18]
(PP Na víně 395 m here vs "E1 cca 190 m" to its southern spur; PP Zebín's zone 641 m vs 670 m; PP Borecké skály
1.7 km vs 1.9 km; PP Libuňka 1.36 km vs 1.6 km).

The ends: A at Ohrazenice joins the D10 from Prague and the I/35 expressway from Liberec (their interchange is
rebuilt by stage 1). B joins the **D35 Úlibice bypass** (D 25.5/100, 1.64 km, planned 2026–2029), which leads to
**D35 Úlibice – Hořice** (D 26.0/130, 16.35 km, under construction since 04/2026) [WD35; ŘSD 29 Apr 2026].

### Known issues

- The 2+1 S 15.25 stretch beyond km 12.4 (20.5 km) is built at four lanes: one category per route (§6).
- No pier stands in a pond (a game rule for all water): the middle stage's line every 1.5 km crosses the pond near
  Stebno lengthwise and is not valid; its reference is bends every 1.0 km.
- A bridge running 400 m along a railway right under it (the last stage's line with bends every 1.0 km, km 7.7–8.1)
  stands its piers on the track: no frame pier's columns miss a railway that close to the axis. Small drags of the
  last stage's starter by up to 100 m: 1 of 72 invalid so (`scripts/small-drag-audit.ts --all --n 6 --radius 100`); by
  up to 25 m none of 48; the middle stage by up to 50 m none of 78.
- The real line tunnels under Pelešany (900 m) and Přepeře (150 m); our Auto finds cuts, bridges and buyouts cheaper
  on the sampled line. Why the real design tunnels there (the houses, the EIA's condition on the landscape) is a cost
  the game does not see.
- The interchanges (eight on the real line) and the II/283 feeder to Semily are not built.
- The ground is ČGS GEOCR50 with IG rajon fallbacks (no cell unmapped); Kozákov's and Trosky's volcanic rocks
  (basanite, nephelinite, pyroclastics) have no GEOCR50 mapping of their own and take the IG rajon's (ig_Vl, ig_Vk).
