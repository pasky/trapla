# Reference routes, candidate scenarios and prior art

*Feasibility mapping; evidence checked from public sources available during research. “UNVERIFIED” means a specific claim/data product was not confirmed at source. Costs are nominal public estimates and not directly comparable (VAT, price year, scope differ).*

## A. Reference geometry, costs and demand

### Motor roads: best current official trail

- **ŘSD map of projects**: [Mapa staveb](https://www.rsd.cz/mapa-staveb/) is the best entry point for current status, schedule, length and project detail pages; the page exposes separate preparation/implementation filters and often a downloadable project leaflet. Its content is searchable/readable but I did **not** verify a public bulk vector download/API of project alignments. Information leaflets commonly contain plan-view alignment maps and structure summaries; vertical profiles/earthwork quantities are project-dependent, not consistently published in the map. Example project landing: [Středočeská D3](https://www.stredoceskad3.cz/), and [ŘSD downloadable maps](https://www.rsd.cz/mapy).
- **GIS services**: ŘSD’s ArcGIS REST service [PrezentaceULS/MapServer](https://geoportal.rsd.cz/arcgis/rest/services/PrezentaceULS/MapServer?f=pjson) was probed: it exposes feature layers for existing network referencing and point locations/descriptions of bridges, underpasses and tunnels—not proposed alignments. [Geoportal map applications](https://geoportal.rsd.cz/web/MapApplication) likewise describe the road network/ULS. **No publicly documented ArcGIS feature service for planned project alignments verified.** Treat leaflet geometry as digitizable reference, not authoritative engineering CAD.
- **Costs/statistics**: project pages/leaflets and [ŘSD map](https://www.rsd.cz/mapa-staveb/) provide route length, estimated cost, schedule and structures at varying detail. Earthwork volumes and longitudinal profiles are **not verified as a consistent open field**; seek EIA/project documentation per route.

### Rail and planning

- **Správa železnic (SŽ)** publishes VRT studies and associated maps through [VRT studies](https://vrtky.cz/ke-stazeni/studie-proveditelnosti) and maintains [VRT GIS portal](https://mapy-ssvrt.opendata.arcgis.com/). Studies (e.g. Praha–Brno–Břeclav) contain alternatives, demand/cost analysis and maps. The GIS hub is real, but downloadable route GIS/KML suitable for reuse was **not verified** in this probe. For project geometry/status see [SŽ](https://www.spravazeleznic.cz/) and project-specific studies. Conventional rail projects are also on SŽ project pages; coverage is not a single open route dataset.
- **EIA**: [CENIA EIA search](https://portal.cenia.cz/eiasea/view/eia100_cr) provides project records and linked documents. For major schemes, EIA documentation typically contains route/variant maps and environmental comparison; the completeness and downloadable underlying GIS vary by case. It is a strong source for competing alternatives, constraints and mitigation, not a normalized engineering-data feed.
- **Regional plans**: ZÚR map corridors can be obtained as documents and in some cases GIS. Verified example: [South Bohemia ZÚR vector data](https://geoportal.kraj-jihocesky.gov.cz/portal/uzemni-planovani/jihocesky-kraj/zasady-uzemniho-rozvoje-jihoceskeho-kraje/data-ze-zur), explicitly 1:100,000/S-JTSK. Other regions have separate portals; availability/licensing and formats are inconsistent. National [PÚR](https://mmr.gov.cz/cs/ministerstvo/stavebni-pravo/koncepce-a-strategie/politika-uzemniho-rozvoje-ceske-republiky) is an adopted planning instrument, not a detailed construction alignment. Regional corridor reservations are broad and should not be mistaken for approved centerlines.

### OSM and traffic

- OSM tags [highway=construction/proposed](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Key:construction), [railway=construction/proposed](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Tag:railway%3Dconstruction) are volunteered mapping, not an official inventory. Proposed coverage is especially incomplete; construction ways can represent only parts, and geometry is generally map-grade rather than design-grade. OSM supports `bridge=*`, `tunnel=*`, `layer=*`, but tagging is inconsistent; no reliable vertical profile or bridge design. **Czech count UNVERIFIED**: Overpass query (counts matching ways, not projects; split routes inflate counts):

```overpass
[out:json][timeout:60];area["ISO3166-1"="CZ"][admin_level=2]->.cz;
(way[highway=construction](area.cz);way[highway=proposed](area.cz);
 way[railway=construction](area.cz);way[railway=proposed](area.cz););
out count;
```

  Probe was blocked by Overpass HTTP 406, so no invented count is reported. Query can be run at [overpass-turbo.eu](https://overpass-turbo.eu/).
- ŘSD publishes [CSD traffic census](https://www.rsd.cz/silnice-a-dalnice/scitani-dopravy): 2020 and 2025 results, including segment-level XLSX, web map and PDFs. The 2020 source says 55,892 km surveyed, with vehicle-class counts; note pandemic-period caveat. **2025 XLSX is linked on the page** (latest page observed) and offers a useful refreshed AADT basis. These are segment counts, not OD matrices. Public national OD matrices/model outputs: **UNVERIFIED**; public studies may report forecasts but not reusable matrices.

## B. Candidate Czech scenarios

Shortlist is based on geometry/constraints and publicly visible project status; cost numbers should be refreshed from the current leaflet before any scenario specification.

| Candidate | Why it is a strong route-comparison case; length/status/cost evidence |
|---|---|
| **D3 Praha–Jílové–Tábor (Central Bohemia)** | Posázaví terrain, Sázava crossings, tunnels/viaducts, protected landscape and long-running route objections make the comparison unusually rich. Three sections total roughly 58–60 km; 2025 government update gives 9.5 km Praha–Jílové at CZK 12.9bn and 15.8 km Jílové–Hostěradice at CZK 17.5bn; other section cost varies by leaflet. Preparation, not all under construction. [MoT 2025 update](https://md.gov.cz/Media/Media-a-tiskove-zpravy/Aktualni-stav-pripravy-stredoceske-D3), [project site](https://www.stredoceskad3.cz/). |
| **D35 Opatovec–Mohelnice** | Roughly 35 km, hilly transition with tunnels/large structures; a high-cost alternative east–west motorway and PPP procurement make earthworks/structures/cost tradeoffs tangible. ŘSD’s 2026 map showed adjacent Litomyšl–Janov/Džbánov–Litomyšl in construction; Opatovec–Mohelnice reported as PPP/preparation, with contemporary total estimate above CZK 30bn (scope/year-sensitive). [ŘSD map](https://www.rsd.cz/mapa-staveb/), [D35 project portal](https://www.dalnice-d35.cz/). |
| **D11 Jaroměř–Trutnov–Poland** | 19.6 km Jaroměř–Trutnov plus 21.2 km border phases; foothill terrain, settlements, cross-border tie-in. ŘSD map observed Jaroměř–Trutnov in realization (2026) and Trutnov–border phases underway; cost by section in leaflets, **combined current cost UNVERIFIED**. [ŘSD map](https://www.rsd.cz/mapa-staveb/). |
| **D52 Pohořelice–Mikulov/Austria** | Approx. 23 km missing link across South Moravian flatlands and the Nové Mlýny water-reservoir/landscape area; crossing design and Austria connection are central competing constraints. Status/cost should be checked per current leaflet; exact current estimate **UNVERIFIED**. [ŘSD map](https://www.rsd.cz/mapa-staveb/), [EIA](https://portal.cenia.cz/eiasea/view/eia100_cr). |
| **D49 Hulín–Fryšták** | Shorter (about 17 km) but contested route/landscape impacts and a long stalled history; useful contrast between route efficiency and environmental/legal constraint. Current status/cost **UNVERIFIED** from primary page in this pass. [ŘSD map](https://www.rsd.cz/mapa-staveb/), [CENIA](https://portal.cenia.cz/eiasea/view/eia100_cr). |
| **D8 Lovosice–Řehlovice / České středohoří** | Approx. 16 km volcanic uplands, protected area, tunnel vs surface alignment controversy; 2013 landslide over the unfinished motorway demonstrates geotechnical risk and mitigation. Now open; historical replay is compelling, but not a current under-construction level. Cost/length documentation: [D8 project](https://www.rsd.cz/mapa-staveb/), [2013 landslide case (Czech Geological Survey)](https://www.geology.cz/). |
| **D0 511 Běchovice–D1** | 12.6 km eastern Prague ring segment; urban edge, settlements, environmental measures and high traffic value. ŘSD map observed 2024–28 implementation; a clear benchmark with published leaflet/map. Current estimated cost should be taken from leaflet. [ŘSD map](https://www.rsd.cz/mapa-staveb/), [D0 511 leaflet](https://www.rsd.cz/documents/38144/80614/rsd-publikace-D0_04-2021.pdf). |
| **D0 518–520 northern Prague ring** | Multi-section urban/peri-urban corridor with competing northern route variants, Vltava crossings and dense settlement; strong alternative comparison. Planning/authorization, not construction; total length/cost vary with selected alternative and are **UNVERIFIED** here. [ŘSD map](https://www.rsd.cz/mapa-staveb/), [CENIA](https://portal.cenia.cz/eiasea/view/eia100_cr). |
| **D1 Říkovice–Přerov** | Final D1 gap, ~10 km; flat but water/floodplain and urban interfaces make alignment/structures and network-completion benefits relevant. Recent construction/opening status and final cost **UNVERIFIED** here; useful historic construction level. [ŘSD map](https://www.rsd.cz/mapa-staveb/). |
| **VRT Praha–Dresden / Krušnohorský tunnel** | Cross-border mountain base tunnel (Czech section reported 23 km, 11.7 km tunnel in project material), exceptionally high tunnel/geology/cost challenge; concept/study and exploration, not full construction. Alternatives and demand are in feasibility materials. [SŽ/MoT tunnel project](https://md.gov.cz/Media/Media-a-tiskove-zpravy/Sprava-zeleznic-a-DB-Netz-vybraly-projektanta-Krus), [VRT studies](https://vrtky.cz/ke-stazeni/studie-proveditelnosti). |
| **VRT Praha–Brno–Břeclav** | Long high-speed corridor with major alternatives and several terrain/urban approach constraints; feasibility studies expose forecasts and costs, ideal for rail alignment but no single fully approved continuous design. [SŽ studies](https://vrtky.cz/ke-stazeni/studie-proveditelnosti). |
| **Praha–Kladno–airport rail** | Suburban corridor with urban tunneling, airport access and existing-corridor constraints; station/route choices and costs are public in project materials. Current works are phased; exact aggregate cost/length **UNVERIFIED**. [SŽ project](https://www.spravazeleznic.cz/stavby-zakazky/prehled-staveb). |

**International comparison cases (3–4):** UK **HS2** (phased, high-cost, route/environment controversies; [official project](https://www.hs2.org.uk/)); **Brenner Base Tunnel** and access routes (Alpine gradients/tunnel interfaces; [BBT](https://www.bbt-se.com/en/)); German **A49** (Dannenröder forest controversy; [project](https://www.deges.de/projekte/projekt/a-49/)); **Stuttgart 21 / Stuttgart–Ulm** (urban underground station plus new-build line; [official](https://www.bahnprojekt-stuttgart-ulm.de/)). Length/cost/status differ by project segment; use official sources for current values.

## C. Prior art and gap

| Product/work | What it already does / data |
|---|---|
| **NIMBY Rails** | Real-world global map, OSM-based geography and editable rail network; focuses on network/service economics, not civil-engineering alignment, terrain-cut/fill, structures or cost realism. [Developer retrospective](https://carloscarrasco.com/nimby-rails-retrospective/). |
| **Transport Fever 2; Cities: Skylines** | Transport/network construction and simulation, but stylized terrain/world and simplified construction economics. TPF2 imports heightmaps via its map editor; community workflows create real-world terrain. [TPF2 map editor](https://wiki.transportfever2.com/doku.php?id=gamemanual:mapeditor), [real-data guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2281524121). Cities: Skylines has community heightmap tools, e.g. [terrain.party](https://terrain.party/) (availability/terms should be rechecked) and [heightmap.skydark.pl](https://heightmap.skydark.pl/). |
| **Rail Route / Train Sim World** | Rail operations/dispatching or driving/building routes, not comparative real-world corridor engineering. [Rail Route](https://railroute.eu/), [Train Sim World](https://dovetailgames.com/games/train-sim-world). |
| **Professional alignment software** | **Trimble Quantm**, **Bentley OpenRoads ConceptStation**, **Autodesk InfraWorks**, **Softree Optimal** support corridor/alignment alternatives and engineering workflows. Commercial, specialist software rather than accessible consumer simulation; check current licensing/pricing. [Quantm](https://www.trimble.com/en/products/quantm), [ConceptStation](https://www.bentley.com/software/openroads-conceptstation/), [InfraWorks](https://www.autodesk.com/products/infraworks/overview), [Softree](https://www.softree.com/). |
| **Research** | GIS + genetic algorithms / multi-objective optimization are established: Jha & Schonfeld, [TRID record](https://trid.trb.org/View/704691); Kim et al. (stepwise GA), [Transportation Research Part B](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0191261504000840); Zhao et al. BIM/GIS, [2019 open article](https://www.mdpi.com/2220-9964/8/4/172). These optimize alignments in research models; practical calibration/data and public-facing playability remain hurdles. |

**Feasibility gap (not a design recommendation):** tools exist at both ends—accessible transport games abstract civil works; engineering packages optimize corridors but are expensive/complex; published GIS studies are not packaged as a public, scenario-based game. Public Czech inputs are unusually promising (ŘSD leaflets, EIA alternatives, traffic counts, ZÚR, SŽ feasibility studies), but are heterogeneous and often PDF-only. A real-world comparison would need legal review of reuse: public accessibility does not itself grant commercial reuse rights; verify each dataset’s explicit license, especially OSM (ODbL attribution/share-alike), third-party study maps, and basemaps.
