# 91 — I/46 Šternberk bypass: curveballs and real constraints

Research note, 2026-11-20. **Evidence limit:** the two late permitting issues are documented in the available reporting. I could not verify a species, mapped meadow, individual pier coordinates, EIA mitigation text, or 2011 decision text from accessible primary documents. Those details are explicitly left unverified rather than guessed. Locations use approximate geographic anchors and the project corridor description; no fabricated point coordinates.

The mission uses the proposed I/46 line (OSM ways 939310904–939310918) in the supplied map extent, south A (49.695386, 17.295375) to north-east B (49.72457, 17.31956). These endpoints are given by the project team; they are not coordinates of the events below.

## Events

### 1. `weisshuhn-villa`
- **Headline:** A bridge pier threatens a historic garden
- **Player line:** News from the site: A protected villa and its garden stand beside the planned bridge pier.
- **Date:** Villa listed as a cultural monument in November 2025; protection proceedings began December 2024. The pier conflict was reported 16 November 2026.
- **Source / quote:** [Zdopravy.cz, 16 Nov 2026](https://zdopravy.cz/nejdriv-historicka-vila-ted-chraneni-motyli-priprava-obchvatu-sternberka-se-znovu-zasekla-307213/): “jeden z pilířů plánovaného mostu stát přímo na zahradě u historického skleníku” — “one of the planned bridge’s piers is to stand directly in the garden by the historic greenhouse.” The article attributes the pier detail to Mladá fronta Dnes. It says the Ministry of Culture listed the villa in November 2025.
- **Where:** Weisshuhn villa, Šternberk; **area only**. The accessible report supplies no street address or coordinates. Monument register number, address, garden boundary and pier location: **UNVERIFIED**; consult [Památkový katalog](https://pamatkovykatalog.cz/) by “Weisshuhnova vila, Šternberk” before assigning a point or radius. The text establishes only the garden beside the historic greenhouse, not a reliably mappable polygon.
- **Rule:** **Keep out of the zone entirely** for the villa and its garden, unless an authoritative conservation decision specifies otherwise. A bridge might avoid ground-level damage, but a pier in the garden is the exact reported conflict; reroute or span the garden without supports. Do not treat the entire neighborhood as forbidden.

### 2. `protected-butterflies`
- **Headline:** Protected butterflies halt the permit
- **Player line:** News from the site: Protected butterflies were found along the route, and the road needs a nature-protection exception.
- **Date:** Found/reported to the authority in September 2026; the ŘSD application for an exception was submitted in late August 2026; permit proceedings suspended until no later than end-September 2027.
- **Source / quote:** [Zdopravy.cz, 16 Nov 2026](https://zdopravy.cz/nejdriv-historicka-vila-ted-chraneni-motyli-priprava-obchvatu-sternberka-se-znovu-zasekla-307213/): “v jeho plánované trase odborníci zjistili výskyt zvláště chráněných motýlů” — “experts found specially protected butterflies in its planned route.” And: “ŘSD musí před povolením stavby získat výjimku ze zákazů u zvláště chráněných druhů.” — “Before the works can be permitted, ŘSD must obtain an exemption from prohibitions concerning specially protected species.”
- **Where:** Somewhere in the construction area / planned route; **area only**. Exact species (including whether it is a *Phengaris* blue), meadow/valley, survey date, occupied habitat polygon, and requested exemption conditions are **UNVERIFIED**. The article and EIA landing page do not identify them. Do not attach a guessed point to Sprchový potok or any named meadow. The exemption is documented as required; its ecological conditions cannot be inferred from the article.
- **Rule:** **Keep out of the zone entirely** as a cautious game rule pending an exemption. Do not assert this is the actual final permit condition: the authority might permit works under seasonal, habitat-restoration or other conditions, but the exemption decision is unavailable here. A bridge is not automatically harmless to a butterfly habitat.

## What really happened

ŘSD’s July 2025 leaflet describes a 4,708 m S 11.5 bypass, including a 287 m viaduct, and estimates **2,037,098,875 CZK excluding VAT**. The EIA record dates the screening conclusion to 20 February 2004; Research note 88 records the zoning decision as December 2011, extended to June 2025, while Zdopravy reports the joint-permit application in June 2024 and suspension until at latest September 2027 after the villa and butterfly issues. The reported plan is to start in 2028 and open in 2030; this is a forecast, not a secured construction date.

Reveal line: **Šternberk’s bypass is designed, but a protected garden and butterflies have stopped the permits for now.**

Sources: [ŘSD leaflet](https://apdos.roadmedia.cz/Upload/Stavby/329/infoletak_s46-sternberk-obchvat.pdf) (“Celková délka úpravy je 4708 metrů”; “Předpokládaná cena stavby: 2 037 098 875 Kč (bez DPH)”); [CENIA OLK068](https://portal.cenia.cz/eiasea/detail/EIA_OLK068) (2004 record and linked dated documents); [Zdopravy, 16 Nov 2026](https://zdopravy.cz/nejdriv-historicka-vila-ted-chraneni-motyli-priprava-obchvatu-sternberka-se-znovu-zasekla-307213/) (permit, suspension, events, reported 2028–2030 schedule); [Research note 88](research/88-town-bypass-candidates.md) (2011 zoning decision and extension; underlying decision not independently rechecked in this note).
