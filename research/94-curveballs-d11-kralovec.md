# Curveballs for D11 1109 and the Trutnov–Královec railway

Research note for possible in-play events. The rules below are **gameplay translations**, not claims that a source imposes a legal design rule. A dated event is documented history; a real place or operating feature is explicitly labelled as such. Coordinates are WGS84 (lat, lon). Where the source gives only a named area, the location is marked approximate/area-only rather than pretending to know an exact footprint.

Scope: the D11 1109 corridor from Trutnov toward the Polish border, and the existing Trutnov–Královec railway. The proposed electric railway is a game proposal; no construction project for it is established in the reviewed sources (see `research/38-railway-missions.md`).
