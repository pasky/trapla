# D35 Opatovec–Mohelnice: curveballs and real outcome

Research note, 2026-11-19. The two playable parts are Opatovec–Staré Město (16.6 km) and Staré Město–Mohelnice (18.23 km). The game map is a simplified corridor, not the final surveyed design. “Curveballs” below distinguish documented D35 events from real site constraints that are not events in the project history. Coordinates are approximate unless explicitly marked otherwise; do not use them as survey geometry.

Rules use the requested choices verbatim. A point/radius is a gameplay trigger envelope, not a claim about an official legal boundary.

## Curveballs

### `pod-skalou-protected-slope` — Protect the yew forest
**News from the site:** A rare yew forest covers the steep slope; keep roadworks out and pass beneath it.

- **Date/source:** EIA documentation, 2010 (archived in CENIA MZP136): [documentation ZIP](https://portal.cenia.cz/eiasea/download/RUlBX01aUDEzNl9kb2t1bWVudGFjZURPQ18xLnppcA/MZP136_dokumentace.zip); cross-check, [ŘSD Opatovec–Staré Město leaflet (09/2025)](https://apdos.roadmedia.cz/Upload/Stavby/53/infoletak_d35-opatovec-st_mesto.pdf).
- **Quote:** “Trasa rychlostní silnice je v místě rezervace vedena tunelem. Toto řešení je optimální” — “The expressway route passes through a tunnel at the reserve. This solution is optimal.” EIA also says the eastern portal must stay in its specified location and portal construction must not affect the forest forming the monument's protection zone.
- **Where:** PP Pod skálou, on the Mladějovský vrch slope above the Dětřichov–Nová Ves area; approx. **49.79, 16.61**, 500 m gameplay trigger radius. **Approximate** point; use official AOPK protected-area polygon for real extent (not this radius). Corridor alignment is reported to cross the monument and its protection zone. Exact polygon: [AOPK open data](https://gis.nature.cz/arcgis/rest/services/Aplikace/Opendata/MapServer), layers for small protected areas and their zones.
- **Rule:** **keep out of the zone entirely** for surface works; tunnel beneath is allowed. This is from this D35's EIA and is the documented reason the line tunnels under the protected slope. Do not treat the trigger radius as the legal boundary.

### `detrichov-tunnel-rock-risk` — The ridge hides bad ground
**News from the site:** Surveyors report faults and weak rock; allow for difficult ground along the long tunnel.

- **Date/source:** ITA-AITES, *Tunel* 4/2023, Dětřichov tunnel design [PDF, pp. 22–32](https://www.ita-aites.cz/files/tunel/2023/4/tunel_2023-4_022-032.pdf); later local reporting on old mine workings and slope risk: [Svitavský deník](https://svitavsky.denik.cz/zpravy-region/stare-stoly-mohou-ztizit-stavbu-tunelu-detrichov-na-d35-u-hrebce-hrozi-sesuv-202.html).
- **Quote:** “Tunel … klesá z 514 m n. m. směrem na východ do 439 m n. m. s nejvyšší výškou nadloží 173 m” — “The tunnel descends eastward from 514 m above sea level to 439 m, with maximum overburden of 173 m.” This verbatim design description is evidence of the deep tunnel setting, not proof of a specific unstable zone.
- **Where:** Dětřichov tunnel, approximately **49.79, 16.61**, corridor-area envelope radius **2 km**; **approximate** tunnel midpoint, not a geological boundary. The EIA/ŘSD project places the 3,983 m tunnel beneath the ridge; the old Hřebeč landslide reported in press is on existing I/35, not established on the new alignment.
- **Rule:** **a budget cap** is not justified; instead **no tunnel / no bridge in the zone** is not supported. This proposed event is a risk/cost modifier rather than one of the four spatial rules, so **do not implement as a hard rule**. Record as a genuine site condition, but the permitted-rule list has no suitable rule; avoid misleading players by converting uncertainty into a no-tunnel constraint.
- **Classification:** Real project geology, but not a newly announced event and no source establishes a discrete unstable-slope polygon intersecting the alignment. **UNVERIFIED** spatial extent and any numeric cost premium.

Nyní v DÚR uvažujeme pouze…” — “The 2016 Valbek study used as the EIA basis considered two variants for the D35 alignment at the Maletín junction. The current detailed design considers only…” (quote continues in the source with the selected variant).
- **Where:** Maletín interchange area, approx. **49.799, 16.783**, **1 km radius**, **approximate**. - **Rule:** **keep out of the zone entirely** applies only if gameplay explicitly represents the abandoned interchange/footprint as a fixed no-build zone; otherwise no listed rule follows from “two options.” Better design event is a player-choice/route variant, not a prohibition. No evidence found of an official municipal veto.
- **Classification:** Documented D35 planning history. The 2019 report discusses Maletín's request/interest in an interchange; it does not establish that the municipality vetoed the road or that the final no-exit outcome was a formal sanction.

- **Classification:** This is a documented D35 environmental-design requirement, not evidence of an animal appearing unexpectedly during construction. It is fair as a curveball only if framed as a later wildlife-agency requirement; that later event is **UNVERIFIED**.

## What really happened

Status and money are time-sensitive. The dated ŘSD leaflets are project estimates, not awarded civil-works prices. PPP availability-payment NPV includes financing, operation and maintenance and must not be called construction cost.

### Mission: Opatovec–Staré Město (16.6 km)
- **Design/estimate:** ŘSD leaflet (state 09/2025) gives 16,600 m, D 26.0/130, and estimated project cost **CZK 17.694 billion** (source: [ŘSD leaflet](https://apdos.roadmedia.cz/Upload/Stavby/53/infoletak_d35-opatovec-st_mesto.pdf)). This is an estimate, not a final contract price; its scope includes an S 11.5/90 connector, so do not treat it as pure motorway cost.
- **EIA:** First corridor section is covered by the earlier D35 EIA history MZP136; the protected-slope/tunnel constraint is documented in its assessment. Exact current validity/permit expiry for this mission: **UNVERIFIED** in the sources reviewed.
- **Procurement/status:** The two sections were bundled into the D35 PPP. Archaeological preparation began on this section in October 2024 (reported 20 Feb 2025, above). In October 2026, Ministry announced selection of ViAlliance35/preferred bidder for the combined PPP; government approval of the draft deal reported 26 Oct 2026. **A separate construction price for this section and a verified works start/opening date are UNVERIFIED.** The project plan cited in 2025 anticipated start 2026 and opening 2029; do not present forecast as achieved status. Sources: [Ministry, preferred-bidder notice](https://md.gov.cz/Media/Media-a-tiskove-zpravy/Ministerstvo-dopravy-vydalo-oznameni-o-vyberu-konc?lang=en-GB), [Ministry PPP decision](https://md.gov.cz/Media/Media-a-tiskove-zpravy/PPP-projektu-na-dalnici-D35-dala-vlada-zelenou-Pr).
- **Reveal line:** *The first 16.6 kilometres are planned with a long tunnel beneath the protected Dětřichov ridge; the PPP winner is selected, but the final section price is not public here.*

### Mission: whole Opatovec–Mohelnice (two sections, about 34.83 km)
- **Design/EIA:** ŘSD's two leaflets report 16.600 km + 18.230 km (second section includes 1.330 km reconstruction of existing D35) and D 26.0/130: [Opatovec–Staré Město](https://apdos.roadmedia.cz/Upload/Stavby/53/infoletak_d35-opatovec-st_mesto.pdf), [Staré Město–Mohelnice](https://apdos.roadmedia.cz/Upload/Stavby/54/infoletak_d35-stare-mesto-mohelnice.pdf). Second-section EIA MZP162: favourable statement published 05 Feb 2018 ([CENIA](https://portal.cenia.cz/eiasea/detail/EIA_MZP162?lang=cs)). All needed building permits reportedly became final in August 2024, last being Maletín tunnel ([Orlický deník, 13 Aug 2024](https://orlicky.denik.cz/zpravy-region/tak-uz-i-tunel-maletin-cela-d35-az-do-mohelnice-ma-pravomocne-stavebni-povoleni/)). Later reporting in Oct 2026 notes a permit extension; the exact extension and section affected should be checked before quoting a current expiry date.
- **Price/status:** Combined PPP covers about **35.4 km** (different length basis from the leaflets) and 22 years of operation/maintenance. ViAlliance35 selected as preferred bidder in Oct 2026; government approval reported 26 Oct 2026. Reported bid **CZK 71.32 billion NPV** is the availability-payment stream for the concession, **not** the construction bill. Sources: [Ministry PPP announcement](https://md.gov.cz/Media/Media-a-tiskove-zpravy/PPP-projektu-na-dalnici-D35-dala-vlada-zelenou-Pr), [Zdopravy 26 Oct 2026](https://zdopravy.cz/vlada-schvalila-nejvetsi-ceskou-dalnicni-zakazku-d35-za-71-miliard-miri-do-snemovny-304719/). The Opatovec–Staré Město leaflet estimate is CZK 17.694bn; the up-to-date Staré Město–Mohelnice estimate in the 09/2025 ŘSD leaflet is **UNVERIFIED here**. Combined civil construction price, contract signature, actual full works start and opening are **UNVERIFIED** from the primary sources retrieved. 2025 articles' 2026 start/2029 opening are forecasts, not completion evidence.
- **Reveal line:** *The two D35 sections passed EIA and permitting and entered one PPP competition; its selected bid covers decades of service as well as construction.*

## Source and evidence cautions

- EIA MZP162's public record explicitly shows favourable statement and dates; do not say Natura 2000 was itself approved as an adverse-impact event. The record says Natura 2000 impact was excluded at screening.
- A 2019 Maletín story about wanting an interchange and an EIA study of two MÚK variants are not proof of a municipal veto, court annulment, or a fight over the Dětřichov tunnel. No Dětřichov-versus-Maletín tunnel variant dispute was found: these are separate tunnel/area topics. The Dětřichov tunnel is part of the Opatovec–Staré Město design; Maletín tunnel and interchange are on Staré Město–Mohelnice.
- The local landslide report concerns old workings / Hřebeč instability; research/73 explicitly notes the reported active Hřebeč landslide is on existing I/35, not the new D35 alignment. No mapped ČGS polygon was verified crossing this alignment, so no landslide exclusion is proposed.
- No verified corridor-specific gas pipeline conflict, newly listed building, protected-nest event, tightened noise limit, or budget cut was found. Do not manufacture one.
