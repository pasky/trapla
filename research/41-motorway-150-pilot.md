## Legal basis

- Act 361/2000 Sb. §18(3) sets the default motorway maximum at **130 km/h** for cars/light vehicles, while “other motor vehicles” are limited to **80 km/h** (§18(3)); §18(7) permits local traffic regulation to raise the §18(3) limit by at most **20 km/h** on a divided motorway. Thus the legal mechanism is an authorized local traffic arrangement and variable speed signs—not a general statutory 150 limit. Heavy vehicles stay at 80; the pilot’s raised limit concerns vehicles whose §18(3) limit can be raised. [§18, Act 361/2000](https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2000-361#p18)
- For a motorway, MD sets local/temporary traffic regulation and use of traffic-information devices under §77(1); §61(2) defines local regulation. A university tunnel-design paper also describes this statutory hierarchy and MD’s motorway role. [Act 361/2000](https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2000-361); [Přibyl, *Maximální dovolená rychlost a její vliv na řešení dálničního tunelu* (2020)](https://www.ita-aites.cz/files/tunel/2020/3/tunel-2020-03_60-69.pdf)
- Legal nuance: §18(7) says “maximum +20 km/h,” which authorizes 150 from a 130 baseline; the signs communicate when that local higher limit is in force. [Act 361/2000 §18(7)](https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2000-361#p18)

## Design speed and geometry

- The 2018 revision of ČSN 73 6101 assigns **130 km/h** design speed to motorway categories including D 27.5 (and D 33.5); the 2018 table tops out at 130, not 150. Source is a university/conference presentation summarizing the standard, rather than the paywalled standard itself. [Radimský et al., “Revize ČSN 73 6101” (conference PDF)](https://konference-projektovani.cz/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/9.pdf); [Přibyl 2020, citing the 2018 revision](https://www.ita-aites.cz/files/tunel/2020/3/tunel-2020-03_60-69.pdf)
- The D3 pilot is an **operational speed-limit change**, not a geometric redesign: the published launch material describes dynamic signs and real-time operating conditions, not reconstruction. Explicit MD statement “geometry unchanged / designed for 130” for this exact section: **UNVERIFIED** in visited sources. Do not represent 150 as a new ČSN design speed. [ODS/MD launch announcement](https://www.ods.cz/clanek/27663-rychlosti-az-150-km-h-mohou-ridici-zkusebne-projet-po-dalnici-d3); [2018 ČSN summary](https://konference-projektovani.cz/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/9.pdf)

### CNOSSOS-EU (EU strategic noise mapping)

- Directive (EU) 2015/996 Annex II establishes CNOSSOS-EU; Directive (EU) 2021/1226 amends it. The road model sums rolling and propulsion source contributions by vehicle category and frequency. [2015/996, EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2015/996/oj); [2021/1226, EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2021/1226/oj)
- CNOSSOS road-source formulations are normally presented for 20–130 km/h, but published discussion of Annex II notes that for speeds **above 130**, the same equation is used with the actual speed (rather than clamping speed to 130). This is a mathematical extrapolation, not validation of the source model/vehicle fleet at 150. [Shilton et al., “Conversion of existing road source data to use CNOSSOS-EU”](https://dgmr.nl/wp-content/uploads/files/Euronoise%20Conversion%20of%20existing%20road%20source%20data%20to%20use%20CNOSSOS-EU%20-%20000564.pdf); [official Directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2015/996/oj)
- In CNOSSOS, the light-vehicle rolling-noise speed term has the form **A + B·lg(v/70)**; the category-1 rolling speed coefficient in the reference-surface formulation is **B = 30 dB**. Holding all else constant, extrapolating that term from 130 to 150 gives:
  - ΔLrolling = 30·lg(150/130) = **+1.91 dB** (about **+1.9 dB**).
  - This applies to the *rolling component*, not automatically to total car pass-by sound: propulsion and rolling powers are combined energetically, with surface, tyre, vehicle and operating conditions affecting the result. It is not a measured D3 increase. [2015/996 Annex II road-source equations/coefficients](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2015/996/oj); [method overview](https://cdn.tii.ie/publications/RE-ENV-07006-01.pdf)
- Heavy vehicles remain limited to 80 km/h under Czech §18(3), so 150 only changes the speed input for eligible light vehicles, not truck speed. [Act 361/2000 §18](https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2000-361#p18)
- D3-pilot acoustic measurements at 150, or a published D3 noise impact study: **UNVERIFIED** in the sources checked. No D3-specific measurement should be implied by the CNOSSOS calculation. [MD launch announcement](https://www.ods.cz/clanek/27663-rychlosti-az-150-km-h-mohou-ridici-zkusebne-projet-po-dalnici-d3); [MD extension announcement](https://md.gov.cz/Media/Media-a-tiskove-zpravy/Pilotni-provoz-rychlosti-150-km-h-na-dalnici-D3-se)

## For the game

Offer an optional **“150 km/h speed limit (pilot)”** only for a motorway with 130-design geometry and an equipped variable-sign / line-control system, where the higher limit can be shown conditionally: dry surface, good visibility, low traffic, no incident (and revert to 130/100 when conditions worsen). This is a game abstraction of the D3 operating conditions, not a new geometry class. [D3 launch conditions](https://www.ods.cz/clanek/27663-rychlosti-az-150-km-h-mohou-ridici-zkusebne-projet-po-dalnici-d3); [130 design-speed summary](https://konference-projektovani.cz/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/9.pdf)

It changes eligible cars’ posted-speed/travel-time calculation and their noise emission; it does **not** change geometric design norms or permit heavy vehicles above 80 km/h. For noise, prefer CNOSSOS-EU’s published speed formula evaluated at actual 150 km/h, while clearly marking it **PROVISIONAL: extrapolation beyond the model’s ordinary 20–130 km/h range, not validated or measured for the D3 pilot**. The +1.9 dB figure is the rolling component only, not a total-road-noise multiplier. [Act 361/2000 §18](https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2000-361#p18); [CNOSSOS/EU source](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2015/996/oj); [speed extrapolation discussion](https://dgmr.nl/wp-content/uploads/files/Euronoise%20Conversion%20of%20existing%20road%20source%20data%20to%20use%20CNOSSOS-EU%20-%20000564.pdf)
