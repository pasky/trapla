## Best-supported candidate: d11, Trutnov–Královec–Lubawka

### What is actually on the table?

- **Existing cross-border service is restored, not merely proposed.** GW Train Regio publishes Trutnov–Královec–Lubawka–Sędzisław passenger timetables for **14 Dec 2025–12 Dec 2026**. This establishes present service, but the page extraction did not expose the timetable PDF's individual daily departures; do not infer a daily train count from it. [GWTR timetable](https://www.gwtr.cz/en/hradec-kralove/timetables/timetable) (visited).
- The verified, granular operating count I found is historical: Královéhradecký kraj's **2022** summer D26 timetable lists **four trains each way per operating day** between Trutnov hl.n. and Sędzisław, via Královec and Lubawka (30 Apr–28 Sep 2022). It is explicitly seasonal, not a present all-year frequency. [KHK timetable PDF/text](https://www.dopravakhk.cz/filedownloads-1227-456b20f0aa98) (visited).
- **Line numbering:** GWTR labels the cross-border service line **043**. Line **047** is Trutnov–Teplice nad Metují, a different line. Avoid calling Trutnov–Lubawka line 047. [GWTR regional page/search result](https://www.gwtr.cz/cs/kralovehradecko); [ČD line 047 timetable](https://www.cd.cz/jizdni-rad/tratove-jizdni-rady/3).
- **Trutnov hl.n.–Královec current time:** the 2022 D26 schedule gives 05:37–06:03, **26 min**, and similar 2022 services; this is a dated observed timetable, not guaranteed current timing. The same 2022 schedule gives Trutnov hl.n.–Lubawka 34 min (05:37–06:11). [KHK D26 timetable](https://www.dopravakhk.cz/filedownloads-1227-456b20f0aa98). Current exact Trutnov–Královec trip time: **UNVERIFIED** from accessible current timetable material.
- **Current infrastructure:** the supplied OSM-based pack description identifies this as single-track and non-electrified; I did not verify a current official section-by-section speed profile, so max speed is **UNVERIFIED** in this note. No substantiated new alignment, electrification, double-tracking or speed-increase scheme specifically for Trutnov–Královec–Lubawka located.
- **2018 Hradec Králové–Trutnov/Broumov feasibility study:** SŽ procured a study for the regional authority's desired system journey times: Hradec Králové–Trutnov **60 min**, Hradec Králové–Svoboda nad Úpou **75 min**, Hradec Králové–Náchod **30 min**, and Hradec Králové–Broumov **75 min**. The stated Trutnov endpoint is not Královec or Lubawka. Procurement value was **CZK 5.5m excl. VAT** (study contract, not construction cost). The study is a studied concept; these are client targets, not evidence of a committed build or adopted target for the border link. [SŽ procurement notice](https://zakazky.spravazeleznic.cz/contract_display_1716.html) (visited).
- **RS5 Praha–Hradec Králové–Wrocław:** SŽ's study commission describes a corridor from Hořany/VRT Polabí to the Czech–Polish border, including Hradec Králové, with a branch to Pardubice; it asks the study to determine how much existing network to use. It does **not** specify a Trutnov–Královec–Lubawka route. A 2025 SŽ announcement on Hradec-region conventional-line schemes says these support enhanced cross-border connections to Wrocław, in context of Jaroměř–Náchod–Meziměstí, not an announced Trutnov-border project. Thus “RS5 via Trutnov–Lubawka” is **UNVERIFIED / not established**; do not present as approved alignment. SŽ's RS5 study procurement value **CZK 12.6m excl. VAT** is the study cost, not construction. [SŽ RS5 study announcement](https://www.spravazeleznic.cz/aktuality/-/asset_publisher/qSujQZ8W3r0i/content/sprava-zeleznic-vybrala-zpracovatele-studie-proveditelnosti-vrt-do-polska?inheritRedirect=false) (visited); [SŽ 2025 regional modernization announcement](https://www.spravazeleznic.cz/web/en/-/modernisation-of-several-lines-in-hradec-kralove-region-has-the-green-light) (visited).
- **Polish side:** PKP PLK's investment intentions document lists works on lines **298/299 Sędzisław–Kamienna Góra–Lubawka**. Passenger trains resumed across the border in 2018, but the material located here does not establish a current new rebuild contract, a design speed, electrification, track count, cost, or construction schedule for this section. Treat detailed “rebuild” specifications as **UNVERIFIED**. [PKP PLK investment intentions 2021–2030](https://www.plk-sa.pl/files/public/user_upload/pdf/Zamierzenia_inwestycyjne/2021-06-30_-_ZAMIERZENIA_INWESTYCYJNE.pdf) (search-result source); [GWTR current timetable](https://www.gwtr.cz/en/hradec-kralove/timetables/timetable).
- **Cost / target gap:** no credible construction-cost estimate, designed speed, electrification system, or future trip-time target for Trutnov–Královec–Lubawka found. Mark all **UNVERIFIED**; don't reuse the RS5 or 2018 study costs as construction budgets.

## Recommendation

Use **d11, Trutnov hl.n.–Královec**, as a modest, accurately labelled *studied regional journey-time* mission—not an RS5 or electrification mission. The documented **60-minute Hradec Králové–Trutnov** goal is real, but is not a target for the short Trutnov–Královec section; don't invent a short-section target. If the gameplay requires a goal wholly inside the pack, leave target speed/time unset or label a chosen game benchmark as a game assumption. Reference the historical four-per-direction summer service only as historical; obtain current PDF departure count before hard-coding service frequency.

**Player-facing text:** “A 2018 feasibility study examined faster regional trains between Hradec Králové and Trutnov, with a 60-minute journey-time goal. This is a studied target, not a committed railway upgrade.”

For a specifically cross-border mission instead: “Passenger trains now cross the border between Trutnov and Sędzisław. No confirmed Trutnov–Královec–Lubawka modernization target was found; improve this existing line as a game proposal.”
## 5. Railway ends on existing tracks: turnout angle (added by the motorway-rail agent, 2026-11-16)

- A new line joins or leaves an existing track through a turnout. The common Czech standard turnout
  **J60-1:12-500** branches off at **4.763642°** (= atan 1/12) with a 500 m diverging radius: geometry table
  quoted in a ČVUT diploma thesis ([core.ac.uk PDF 84832728](https://core.ac.uk/download/pdf/84832728.pdf),
  search excerpt "J60-1:12-500-I. 60. 4,763 642. 500."); SŽ's turnout rules are SŽDC S3 díl IX
  ([vlaky.net copy](https://www.vlaky.net/upload/images/reports/007386/SZDC_S3_Dil_09_sezm1az3_20190301.pdf),
  which notes that for some standard turnouts the stated ratio is not exactly the tangent of the angle) and
  slimmer turnouts branch at less (1:18.5, 1:26.5, 1:33.5; DT Prostějov / SŽ conference papers).
- Game rule (data/railway.ts `TURNOUT_ANGLE_RAD`, checks.ts `trackEndViolations`): a railway end in a
  mission station, or on a mapped line it branches off, leaves that track at no more than 4.76°; the
  station's track direction is the principal axis of the mapped railway cells within 100 m of the end (5 m
  raster; at Trutnov hl.n. −45.7°, at Královec 46.8°, stable within 1° for radii 60–150 m). Rebuilt
  stretches of a mapped line are not checked at their own ends (they lie on the line they replace).
- Finding: the first Královec starter left Trutnov hl.n. at ≈ 80° to its tracks (Královec end ≈ 20°).
